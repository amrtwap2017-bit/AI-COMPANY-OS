## Validation Checklist

### Market Validation

- [ ] Egypt hospitality market size confirmed: USD 21.54B (2026)
- [ ] 143 hotels / 33,926 rooms pipeline verified as target
- [ ] 7.12% CAGR confirmed as growth trajectory
- [ ] No direct competitor with hospitality-specific engineering platform
- [ ] Pilot customers identified and interested

### Business Model Validation

- [ ] Value proposition resonates with pilot customers
- [ ] Pricing tiers (EGP 3,000/7,500/15,000) acceptable to target market
- [ ] Subscription model preferred over transaction-based
- [ ] Willingness to pay confirmed for pilot scope
- [ ] Contract length (monthly/annual) acceptable

### Operational Validation

- [ ] Business can operate with 1 customer
- [ ] Business can scale to 10 customers
- [ ] Business can scale to 50 customers
- [ ] Customer acquisition channels identified
- [ ] Support model defined for each tier
