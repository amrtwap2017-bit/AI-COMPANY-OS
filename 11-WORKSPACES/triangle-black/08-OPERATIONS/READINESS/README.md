# Phase 08 — Enterprise Operational Readiness

> Transforming Triangle Black from a software project into a company capable of operating software.

## Mission

Verify that every business capability designed during Phases 0–7 is operationally executable. This phase is not about coding. This phase is about proving that the entire enterprise can function.

## What This Phase Is Not

- ❌ Not software engineering
- ❌ Not product design
- ❌ Not architecture redesign
- ❌ Not business workflow redesign
- ❌ Not API redesign

## What This Phase Is

- ✅ Operational process validation
- ✅ Business readiness verification
- ✅ Customer success preparation
- ✅ Security and compliance audit
- ✅ Infrastructure hardening
- ✅ Team and support readiness
- ✅ Financial validation
- ✅ AI governance establishment
- ✅ Go-live planning and execution readiness
