## UAT Plan

| Phase | Activity | Duration | Participants |
|-------|----------|----------|-------------|
| Preparation | Define test scenarios, prepare data, train UAT team | 1 week | QA + Product |
| Execution | Run UAT scripts, document results | 2 weeks | Pilot customers |
| Feedback | Collect feedback, prioritize issues | 1 week | Product Owner |
| Remediation | Fix critical issues, retest | 1 week | Engineering |
| Sign-off | Customer signs off on UAT | 1 day | Stakeholders |
