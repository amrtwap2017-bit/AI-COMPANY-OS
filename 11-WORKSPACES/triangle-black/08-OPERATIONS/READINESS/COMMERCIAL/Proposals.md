## Proposal Template

```
[Triangle Black Logo]

PROPOSAL FOR: [CUSTOMER NAME]
DATE: [DATE]
PREPARED BY: [SALES REP NAME]

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
   [2-3 sentence summary of problem and solution]

2. CUSTOMER NEEDS
   [List of identified pain points and requirements]

3. SOLUTION OVERVIEW
   [Product description, modules, integrations]

4. IMPLEMENTATION PLAN
   [Timeline, milestones, responsibilities]

5. PRICING
   [Monthly subscription + setup fees + optional services]

6. TERMS AND CONDITIONS
   [Payment terms, contract length, SLA, termination]

7. NEXT STEPS
   [Acceptance process, signature, kickoff]

---

SIGNATURE: _____________________ DATE: _____________
[CUSTOMER NAME]

SIGNATURE: _____________________ DATE: _____________
[TRIANGLE BLACK]
```

## Proposal Types

| Type | Content | Length | Use Case |
|------|---------|--------|----------|
| Quick Quote | Pricing only | 1 page | Small hotel, price-sensitive |
| Standard | Full proposal | 5-10 pages | Mid-size hotel |
| Enterprise | Custom proposal | 10-20 pages | Hotel chain, complex needs |

## Proposal Process

1. Demo completed → needs understood
2. Pricing calculated based on room count + modules
3. Proposal drafted using template
4. Reviewed by COO (if > $500/mo)
5. Sent to customer via email
6. Follow-up call scheduled within 3 business days
