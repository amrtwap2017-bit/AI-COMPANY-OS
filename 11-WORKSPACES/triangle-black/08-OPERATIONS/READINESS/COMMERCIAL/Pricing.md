## Discounts

| Condition | Discount | Notes |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| Annual payment | 2 months free | 16.7% discount |
| Referral | 1 month free | Referred customer signs up |
| Non-profit | 15% | Verified non-profit hotels |
| Pilot program | 50% off first 3 months | Limited to 5 customers |

## Payment Terms

- Monthly: Due on 1st of each month
- Annual: Due at contract signing
- Setup fee: Due at contract signing
- Late payment: 1.5% monthly interest
- Payment methods: Bank transfer, credit card
