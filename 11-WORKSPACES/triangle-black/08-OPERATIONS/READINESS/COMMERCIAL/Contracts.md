## Contract Types

| Type | Duration | Auto-Renew | Termination | Use Case |
|------|----------|------------|-------------|----------|
| Monthly | Month-to-month | Yes | 30 days notice | Starter/Growth tiers |
| Annual | 12 months | Yes | 30 days before renewal | Professional/Enterprise |
| Pilot | 3 months | No | At end of pilot | New market entry |

## Contract Sections

1. **Parties** — Legal names of both parties
2. **Services** — Description of platform and modules
3. **Fees** — Pricing, payment terms, late fees
4. **Term** — Start date, duration, renewal
5. **SLA** — Service level commitments
6. **Data** — Data ownership, privacy, security
7. **Confidentiality** — NDA terms
8. **Limitation of Liability** — Cap on damages
9. **Termination** — Conditions and notice periods
10. **Governing Law** — Egyptian law, Cairo courts
