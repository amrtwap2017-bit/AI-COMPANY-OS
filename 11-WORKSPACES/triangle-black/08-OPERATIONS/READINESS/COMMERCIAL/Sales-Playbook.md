## Target Customer Profiles (TCP)

### TCP 1: Independent Hotel
- **Size:** < 50 rooms
- **Pain point:** Manual processes, no PMS
- **Budget:** $50-100/mo
- **Decision maker:** Owner/manager
- **Sales approach:** Value-based, ease of use
- **Close rate target:** 30%

### TCP 2: Mid-Size Hotel
- **Size:** 50-200 rooms
- **Pain point:** Outdated PMS, high costs
- **Budget:** $200-500/mo
- **Decision maker:** General Manager
- **Sales approach:** ROI-driven, migration support
- **Close rate target:** 25%

### TCP 3: Hotel Chain
- **Size:** 200+ rooms, multiple properties
- **Pain point:** Fragmented systems, no central reporting
- **Budget:** $1,000-3,000/mo
- **Decision maker:** Corporate Operations Director
- **Sales approach:** Enterprise-grade, multi-property
- **Close rate target:** 15%

## Sales Objections

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We already have a system" | Compare features, show migration path |
| "Too expensive" | Show ROI calculation, offer annual discount |
| "Too small for us" | Highlight scalability, reference similar hotels |
| "Data security concerns" | Share security documentation, compliance certs |
| "Need local support" | Ensure Arabic-speaking support team |
