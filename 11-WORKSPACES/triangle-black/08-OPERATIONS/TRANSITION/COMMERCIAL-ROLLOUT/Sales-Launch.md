## Sales Launch Phases

```
SILENT ──► SOFT ──► PUBLIC
  │        │        │
  Internal Pilot   Marketing
  + first    hotels    + outreach
  customer  + refine  + full sales
```

## Phase 1: Silent Launch (Week 1-2)

- Platform live but not publicly announced
- First customer onboarding
- Sales materials finalized
- Demo environment ready
- Sales team trained

## Phase 2: Soft Launch (Week 3-4)

- Up to 5 pilot hotels
- Referral-only customer acquisition
- Pricing at pilot discount (50% off)
- Feedback collection and iteration
- Case study development

## Phase 3: Public Launch (Month 2+)

- Marketing campaign begins
- Hospitality industry outreach
- Full pricing active
- Partner program announced
- Public website updated
