## Partner Types

| Type | Description | Commission | Requirements |
|------|-------------|------------|--------------|
| Referral Partner | Refers hotels to Triangle Black | 10% first year MRR | Industry connection |
| Implementation Partner | Onboards hotels for a fee | Setup fee share | Technical capability |
| Technology Partner | Integrates with Triangle Black | Revenue share | Existing integration |
| Reseller | Resells Triangle Black under own brand | 20% margin | Sales team |

## Partner Program Structure (V1)

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Launch date | Month 3 (post-launch) |
| Referral fee | 10% of MRR for 12 months |
| Payout threshold | $50 minimum |
| Payout frequency | Quarterly |
| Partner portal | Manual (spreadsheet) V1, automated V2 |
