## Invoicing Process

1. Customer contract signed
2. Invoice generated on billing date
3. Invoice sent via email (PDF)
4. Payment tracked (bank transfer / card)
5. Payment applied to customer account
6. Revenue recorded in books

## Revenue Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| MRR | Monthly Recurring Revenue | Growing |
| ARPU | Average Revenue Per Unit | Increasing |
| LTV | Lifetime Value | > 12× CAC |
| CAC | Customer Acquisition Cost | < $50 |
| Churn Rate | % of customers lost | < 5% monthly |
| Net Revenue Retention | Revenue from existing customers | > 100% |
| Days Sales Outstanding | Avg days to collect | < 30 days |
