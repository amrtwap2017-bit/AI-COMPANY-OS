## Transition Procedure

For each process:
1. **Document** — Process is documented (source phase)
2. **Validate** — Process works in practice
3. **Train** — New owner trained on process
4. **Shadow** — Old owner shadows for 1 week
5. **Handover** — Ownership formally transferred
6. **Monitor** — New owner operates for 2 weeks
7. **Sign-off** — Transition complete
