## Activation Process

For each SOP:
1. **Finalize** — SOP content approved
2. **Train** — Owner trained on SOP
3. **Test** — SOP performed in staging environment
4. **Activate** — SOP becomes live procedure
5. **Verify** — SOP performed correctly in production
6. **Audit** — SOP compliance checked monthly
