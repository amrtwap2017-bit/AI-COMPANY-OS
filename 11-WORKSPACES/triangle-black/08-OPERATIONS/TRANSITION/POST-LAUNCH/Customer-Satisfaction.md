| Method | Frequency | Sample | Target |
|--------|-----------|--------|--------|

## At-Risk Customer Identification

| Indicator | Threshold | Action |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| NPS score < 0 | Detractor | COO outreach within 24 hours |
| CSAT < 3.0 | Dissatisfied | Root cause analysis |
| Support tickets > 10/month | Frequent issues | Account review |
| Usage declining > 20% | Disengagement | Training refresher |
| Payment overdue > 30 days | At-risk churn | Payment reminder + outreach |
