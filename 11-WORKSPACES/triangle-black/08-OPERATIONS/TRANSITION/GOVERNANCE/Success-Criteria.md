## Go-Live Success Criteria

| # | Criterion | Metric | Target | Owner |
|---|-----------|--------|--------|-------|
| 1 | Production deployment | Platform accessible via HTTPS | ✅ Working | DevOps |
| 2 | First customer live | Customer using platform daily | ✅ Active | COO |
| 3 | Platform stability | No SEV-1 incidents | 0 incidents | CTO |
| 4 | Customer satisfaction | NPS or satisfaction score | > 4.0/5 | COO |
| 5 | Support responsiveness | First response time | < 4 hours | Support |
| 6 | Revenue generation | First payment received | > $0 | Finance |
| 7 | Data integrity | No data loss incidents | 0 incidents | CTO |
| 8 | Security posture | No breach or unauthorized access | 0 incidents | CTO |
