## Stakeholder Map

| Stakeholder | Interest | Communication | Frequency | Owner |
|-------------|----------|---------------|-----------|-------|
| Executive Committee | Strategic | Dashboard, report | Weekly | CTO |
| Team (internal) | Operational | Standup, Slack | Daily | CTO |
| First customer | Experience | Email, call | Daily (hypercare) | COO |
| Future customers | Sales | Newsletter, blog | Monthly | Sales |
| Partners | Relationship | Email | Per event | COO |
| Investors (if any) | ROI | Quarterly update | Quarterly | CEO |

## Communication Channels

| Channel | Purpose | Audience |
|---------|---------|----------|
| Slack (#transitions) | Daily ops, blockers | Team |
| Email | Formal updates, approvals | All |
| Weekly Sync (Google Meet) | Progress review | Team |
| Executive Report (email) | Status to execs | Executive |
| In-app notification | Platform status | Customers |
| Status page | Public uptime | All |
