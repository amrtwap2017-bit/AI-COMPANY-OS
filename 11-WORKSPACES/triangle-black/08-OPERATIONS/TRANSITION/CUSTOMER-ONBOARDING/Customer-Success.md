## Customer Success Lifecycle

```
Onboard ──► Adopt ──► Retain ──► Expand ──► Advocate
   │         │         │          │          │
  Day 1-14  Week 2-4  Month 2+    Month 3+   Month 6+
  Setup +   Active     Regular    Upsell /   Referral
  Training  Usage      Check-ins  Cross-sell
```

## Customer Success Touchpoints

| Time | Touchpoint | Owner | Goal |
|------|-----------|-------|------|
| Day 0 | Welcome email | COO | Set expectations |
| Day 1 | Kickoff call | COO | Align on plan |
| Day 3 | Training day 1 | Support | Capability transfer |
| Day 7 | Go-live day | COO | Launch |
| Day 14 | First check-in | COO | Satisfaction check |
| Day 30 | Monthly review | COO | KPI review |
| Day 60 | Business review | COO | Value realization |
| Day 90 | Quarterly review | COO + CTO | Roadmap alignment |

## Customer Health Scores

| Dimension | Metric | Weight | Good (3) | OK (2) | At Risk (1) |
|-----------|--------|--------|----------|--------|-------------|
| Usage | Daily active users | 25% | > 80% | 50-80% | < 50% |
| Engagement | Feature adoption | 20% | > 70% | 40-70% | < 40% |
| Support | Ticket volume | 15% | < 5/mo | 5-15/mo | > 15/mo |
| Satisfaction | NPS / CSAT | 25% | > 4.0 | 3.0-4.0 | < 3.0 |
| Business | Revenue health | 15% | On time | Late < 30d | Late > 30d |

## Customer Success Playbook

| Scenario | Play | Owner |
|----------|------|-------|
| Usage drops | Reach out, offer training | COO |
| Support tickets high | Root cause analysis, training | Support Lead |
| Renewal approaching | Schedule business review | COO |
| Feature request received | Log in roadmap, follow up | COO |
| At-risk customer | Executive intervention | CTO + COO |
