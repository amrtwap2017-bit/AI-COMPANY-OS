## First Client Selection Criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Ideal Profile |
|-----------|--------|---------------|
| Relationship | High | Existing connection, trust |
| Technical capability | High | Willing to test and provide feedback |
| Hotel size | Medium | < 100 rooms (manageable scope) |
| Location | Medium | Cairo area (onsite support) |
| Contract flexibility | Low | Month-to-month preferred |
| Budget | Low | Starter or Growth tier |

## First Client Experience

### Pre-Launch
- Welcome email from CTO + COO
- Kickoff call (30 min)
- Technical questionnaire
- Timeline agreement

### Provisioning
- Tenant created (schema + subdomain)
- Admin accounts created
- Hotel profile configured
- Room types and inventory loaded
- Rate plans configured

### Training
- Admin training (2 hours, remote)
- Staff training (2 hours, remote)
- User guides provided
- Sandbox access for practice

### Go-Live
- Production access granted
- Verification call
- Support contacts shared
- Hypercare begins
