## Standup Format

```
1. What happened since last standup?
   - Incidents: [count and status]
   - Tickets resolved: [count]
   - Tickets opened: [count]

2. What are the top priorities today?
   - Priority 1: [owner]
   - Priority 2: [owner]
   - Priority 3: [owner]

3. Any blockers?
   - Blocker 1: [owner + resolution]
   - Blocker 2: [owner + resolution]

4. Customer updates?
   - Customer name: [status]
   - Customer name: [status]

5. Action items
   - Action 1: [owner] by [time]
   - Action 2: [owner] by [time]
```

## Standup Rules

1. Start on time, end on time
2. No deep-dives during standup (defer to breakout)
3. Every team member speaks
4. Blocker resolution is top priority
5. Action items assigned with clear owner
