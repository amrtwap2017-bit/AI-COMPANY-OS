## Hypercare Feedback Channels

| Channel | Frequency | Duration | Format |
|---------|-----------|----------|--------|
| Daily check-in call | Daily | 15 min | Phone / video |
| End-of-day email | Daily | — | Email summary |
| Weekly satisfaction survey | Weekly | 5 min | Online form |
| Hypercare exit survey | Once | 10 min | Online form |
| Direct feedback (Slack) | Continuous | — | Chat |

## Daily Check-In Script

```
1. How was your day using the platform?
   - What worked well?
   - What was frustrating?

2. Any issues today?
   - Were they resolved to your satisfaction?
   - Any lingering concerns?

3. Anything you need help with?
   - Training? Configuration? Questions?

4. Any feature requests?
   - What would make your job easier?

5. Any other feedback?
   - General comments, suggestions, concerns
```
