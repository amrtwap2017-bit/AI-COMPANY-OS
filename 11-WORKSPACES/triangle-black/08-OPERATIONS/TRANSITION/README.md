# PHASE-09 — Enterprise Transition & Go-Live

> Transitioning Triangle Black from implementation to live operation.

## Mission

Create a complete Enterprise Transition & Go-Live Program that ensures Triangle Black can launch successfully, onboard its first customers, operate safely, measure success, and continuously improve — with minimum business risk.

## Core Principles

1. **Business Continuity** — Never disrupt live operations
2. **Operational Excellence** — Every process documented and trained
3. **Customer Success** — First client experience defines the brand
4. **Controlled Rollout** — Phased go-live with measurable gates
5. **Risk Mitigation** — Every transition step has a rollback
6. **Observability** — Production visibility from day one
7. **Continuous Feedback** — Customer and team input drives iteration
8. **Scalability** — V1 foundation must support V2 growth
9. **Cost Efficiency** — $6-40/mo VPS budget respected
10. **Hospitality Best Practices** — Domain-driven, not tech-driven
11. **No Vendor Lock-in** — Open source stack, portable data
12. **AI-Assisted Operations** — Rule-based AI from day one
