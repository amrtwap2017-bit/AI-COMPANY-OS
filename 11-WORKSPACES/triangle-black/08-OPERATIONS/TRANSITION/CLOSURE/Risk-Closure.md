## Risk Closure Process

```
Review each risk ──► Assess current status ──► Close or transfer ──► Document
     │                     │                        │                  │
  Risk register         Is risk still           If resolved →      Risk log
  from Phase 9          relevant?               Close              updated
                        If yes → transfer       If transferred →
                        to Phase 10             New Phase 10 owner
```

## Risk Closure Criteria

| Decision | Criteria | Action |
|----------|----------|--------|
| CLOSED | Risk resolved or no longer relevant | Document resolution, archive |
| TRANSFERRED | Risk still relevant | Move to Phase 10 risk register |
| ACCEPTED | Risk accepted as ongoing | Document acceptance, monitor |

## Post-Transition Risk Register

Risks transferred to Phase 10 are documented in the ongoing risk register maintained by the CTO.
