## Support KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Target | Measurement | Current |
|-----|-----------|--------|-------------|---------|
| First Response Time | Time from ticket creation to first response | < 4 hours | Average across all tickets | — |
| Resolution Time | Time from ticket creation to resolution | < 24 hours (SEV-2) | Average by severity | — |
| SLA Compliance % | % of tickets meeting SLA | > 95% | Monthly | — |
| Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) | Post-ticket survey score | > 4.0/5 | Average monthly | — |
| Tickets per Customer | Support tickets per customer per month | < 10 | Monthly average | — |
| First Contact Resolution | % resolved on first contact | > 70% | Monthly | — |
| Backlog Size | Open tickets at end of day | < 20 | Daily count | — |
| Reopened Tickets | % of tickets reopened within 7 days | < 5% | Monthly | — |

## KPI Review Cadence

| KPI | Review | Owner | Action if Below Target |
|-----|--------|-------|-----------------------|
| First Response Time | Weekly | Support Lead | Add staff, improve triage |
| Resolution Time | Weekly | Support Lead | Escalate faster, improve knowledge base |
| SLA Compliance | Monthly | COO | Process improvement |
| CSAT | Monthly | COO | Training, follow-up |
| Tickets per Customer | Monthly | COO | Customer health check |
| FCR | Monthly | Support Lead | Knowledge base improvement |
| Backlog | Daily | Support Lead | Re-prioritize, escalate |
| Reopened Rate | Monthly | Support Lead | Improve resolution quality |
