## Escalation Rules

1. Level 1 has 30 min to resolve before escalating
2. Each level has 1 hour to resolve before escalating further
3. Any issue flagged as SEV-1 bypasses Level 1 and 2
4. All escalations are logged in #incidents
5. Customer-facing escalations require COO approval
