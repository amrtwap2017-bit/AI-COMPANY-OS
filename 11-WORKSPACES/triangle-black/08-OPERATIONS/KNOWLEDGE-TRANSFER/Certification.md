## Certification Levels

| Level | Title | Audience | Prerequisites | Validity |
|-------|-------|----------|---------------|----------|
| L1 | Triangle Black User | Front desk, housekeeping | None | 1 year |
| L2 | Triangle Black Administrator | Hotel admins | L1 | 1 year |

| L4 | Triangle Black Expert | Partners, internal team | L3 | 2 years |

## Certification Requirements

### L1 — User
- Complete platform overview training (TRN-001)
- Pass quiz (80% score)
- Demonstrate: login, create reservation, check-in/out

### L2 — Administrator
- Hold L1 certification
- Complete admin training (TRN-003)
- Pass quiz (85% score)
- Demonstrate: user management, configuration, reporting

### L3 — Professional
- Hold L2 certification
- Complete advanced training
- Pass quiz (90% score)
- Demonstrate: multi-property management, integrations

### L4 — Expert
- Hold L3 certification
- Complete trainer training
- Pass final assessment
- Demonstrate: ability to train others
