## Article Template

```
Title: [Short descriptive title]
Category: [Customer-Facing | Internal | Product]
Tags: [comma, separated, keywords]
Last Updated: [Date]
Owner: [Team name]

## Overview
[Brief description of what this article covers]

## Steps
1. [Step one]
2. [Step two]

## Related Articles
- [Link to related article 1]
- [Link to related article 2]
```

## Knowledge Base Rules

- Articles reviewed quarterly for accuracy
- Stale articles flagged after 6 months without update
- Customer-facing articles in English + Arabic
- Screenshots updated with each UI change
- Internal runbooks kept in PHASE-08
