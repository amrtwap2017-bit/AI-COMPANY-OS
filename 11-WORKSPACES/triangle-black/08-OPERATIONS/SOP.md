## SOP Structure

Each SOP follows this format:
1. **Purpose** — Why this procedure exists
2. **Scope** — When to apply
3. **Prerequisites** — What's needed before starting
4. **Steps** — Numbered, actionable instructions
5. **Expected Outcome** — What success looks like
6. **Troubleshooting** — Common issues and resolutions
7. **Escalation** — Who to contact if stuck
