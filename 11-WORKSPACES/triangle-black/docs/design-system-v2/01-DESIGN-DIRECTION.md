# Triangle Black Design System v2
# Design Direction

## Product Identity

Triangle Black is an Enterprise AI Operations Platform.
Primary use: hotel engineering, asset management, maintenance, procurement, operational intelligence.
Primary users: operations managers, engineers, procurement specialists, executives.

## Design Principle

"An enterprise operating system engineered for hospitality and asset intelligence."

The system must feel:
- Serious — communicates trust and reliability
- Controlled — precise, not decorative
- Premium — refined without being luxurious
- Technical — engineered aesthetic
- Trustworthy — every decision traceable

## Visual Philosophy

LIGHT-FIRST operational interface.
DARK command elements for navigation and executive contexts.
BRONZE accent for brand identity only.

## What This Is NOT

- Not a luxury hotel website
- Not a generic AI dashboard
- Not a dark SaaS template
- Not a copy of any product
- Not a marketing website

## Primary Experience Modes

OPERATIONAL WORKSPACE (default)
- Very light neutral background #F4F4F2
- White card surfaces
- Deep charcoal typography
- Subtle neutral borders
- High information density

COMMAND NAVIGATION (sidebar)
- Neutral graphite #1C1C1E
- Not brown, not pure black
- High contrast text
- Bronze accent for active state only

EXECUTIVE COMMAND CENTER
- May use darker surfaces
- KPI-first hierarchy
- Whitespace creates authority

AI INTELLIGENCE LAYER
- Controlled recommendation pattern
- Evidence-first
- Not ChatGPT aesthetic

## Core Design Questions Every Screen Answers

1. Where am I?
2. What am I looking at?
3. What needs attention?
4. What can I do?
5. What happened?
6. Why did it happen?
7. What evidence supports it?
8. What is the next action?
