# TRIANGLE BLACK — 5-Minute Customer Demo Script

## CORE MESSAGE
"We turn fragmented hotel engineering data into measurable operational decisions."

---

## THE STORY (5 minutes, 5 acts)

### Act 1: The Problem (30 seconds)
"Your engineering team manages hundreds of assets, thousands of work orders,
and a PM schedule — across spreadsheets, WhatsApp, and paper.
You can't see what's critical until it breaks.
You can't prove your team's value to the hotel GM.
You can't answer: where should we focus today?"

### Act 2: The Data (60 seconds)
SHOW: Dashboard → Data Quality section
"Here's your operational truth. We imported your data.
We classified it. We show you exactly what we know,
how confident we are, and where the gaps are.
No fake precision."

POINT TO: WO→Asset linkage %, PM compliance %, Data confidence levels

### Act 3: The Attention (60 seconds)
SHOW: Attention command center
"Every morning, your chief engineer sees this.
P0 = Act now. P1 = Act today. P2 = Schedule this week.
No spreadsheet. No WhatsApp group. One screen."

CLICK: First P0 item → show evidence + impact + recommended action

### Act 4: The Intelligence (90 seconds)
SHOW: Recommendations page
"AI analyzed 3 months of your WO history and PM records.
Here's what it found."

CLICK: One recommendation (e.g., AHU bearing pattern)
SHOW: Evidence → Confidence → Recommended action → Human approve button

"The engineer reads the evidence. Makes the decision. Approves.
Triangle Black never acts automatically. The human decides."

CLICK: Approve → Record outcome 2 weeks later
SHOW: Outcome recorded → ROI calculated

### Act 5: The Proof (60 seconds)
SHOW: Pilot dashboard → ROI section
"After 30 days with one hotel, here's what we can prove:
- PM compliance: 71% → 89%
- Critical WO response: 6.2h → 2.1h
- Verified avoided cost: EGP 47,000

That's what we put in the executive report."

SHOW: PDF report button

"This is what you show your hotel GM.
This is what justifies your engineering company's value."

---

## CLOSING LINE
"Let us run 30 days with one of your hotels.
We import your data. We set a baseline.
At the end of 30 days, you decide if the numbers justify the contract.
No risk. We prove it first."

---

## DEMO RULES
- Never show 930 API routes
- Never show 175 database tables
- Never show test counts
- Never show technical architecture
- Show ONE hotel, ONE problem, ONE recommendation, ONE outcome
- Keep it under 5 minutes
- End with the customer's specific pain point
