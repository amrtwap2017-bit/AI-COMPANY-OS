# Triangle Black — Customer Success Framework

## Feedback → Product Loop
Customer Signal ↓ Classification (P0/P1/P2/P3) ↓ Product Backlog ↓ Sprint Planning ↓ Implementation ↓ Release ↓ Customer Validation


## Signal Categories

| Priority | Type | Example | Response |
|----------|------|---------|----------|
| P0 | Blocking | Login fails, data loss | < 24h |
| P1 | Critical product | Missing KPI, broken workflow | < 1 week |
| P2 | Quality | UX improvement, slow load | Next sprint |
| P3 | Future | New integration, mobile app | Roadmap |

## Customer Health Signals

Track monthly per customer:
- Login frequency (weekly active users)
- WO completion rate trend
- PM compliance trend
- Recommendation approval rate
- ROI snapshot frequency

## Pilot → Conversion Criteria

Customer converts from pilot when:
1. At least 2 KPI snapshots captured (before + after)
2. At least 1 AI recommendation approved
3. PM compliance improving trend visible
4. Team actively using work order workflow
5. Monthly executive report generated

## Reference Customer Template

After successful pilot:
- Case study: before/after KPI improvement
- Video testimonial: operations director
- ROI calculation: documented cost avoidance
- Reference call: available for prospects
