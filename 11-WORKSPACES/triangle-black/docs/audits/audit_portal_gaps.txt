*   `/operations/sites/[id]/page.tsx` (Operations)

#### **High Priority P1: Missing Edit/Create Pages**

*   `portal/app/(app)/quotes/new/page.tsx` & `/[id]/edit/page.tsx` (App)
    *   *Impact:* Quoting process is manual; no direct UI for creating or modifying quotes from a list view.

#### **Medium Priority P2: Missing Detail Pages**

*   `portal/app/(enterprise)/operations/sites/new/page.tsx` & `[id]` detail.
