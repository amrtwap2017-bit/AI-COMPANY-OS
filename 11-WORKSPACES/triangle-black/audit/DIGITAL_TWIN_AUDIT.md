# Digital Twin Audit

A full operational graph spanning Organisation→Portfolio→Hotel→Department→Location→Asset→Component→Work Order→PM→Supplier→Procurement→Cost→Outcome is NOT VERIFIED: no live schema/data was available, active OpenAPI is broken, and relationship completeness was not established.

Classification: partially implemented relational/presentation capability; not verified as an operational digital twin.
