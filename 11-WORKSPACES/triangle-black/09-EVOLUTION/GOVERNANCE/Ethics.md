## Core Ethics Principles

1. **Integrity** — Do the right thing, always
2. **Transparency** — Be open about decisions and data
3. **Fairness** — Treat all customers, partners, and employees fairly
4. **Accountability** — Own mistakes and learn from them
5. **Privacy** — Protect customer and employee data
6. **Inclusivity** — Build for everyone, regardless of background
7. **Sustainability** — Consider environmental impact

## AI Ethics

| Principle | Implementation |
|-----------|---------------|
| Human oversight | AI assists, humans decide |
| Transparency | Users know when AI is involved |
| Fairness | No bias in AI decisions |
| Accountability | AI failures have clear owners |
| Privacy | Customer data protected |
| Explainability | AI decisions can be explained |

## Business Ethics

| Area | Policy |
|------|--------|
| Conflicts of interest | Declare and recuse |
| Gifts and entertainment | < $50 value, no cash |
| Anti-corruption | Zero tolerance |
| Insider trading | Policy and training |
| Competitive intelligence | Legal methods only |
| Customer data | Owned by customer, not us |
| Partner relationships | Transparent, fair dealing |
