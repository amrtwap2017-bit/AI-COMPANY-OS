## AI Governance Maturity

```
L1: BASIC (V1)                      L2: STRUCTURED (H1)
├── Acceptable use policy            ├── Prompt governance
├── Basic evaluation                 ├── AI ethics framework
├── No prompt management             ├── Evaluation pipeline
└── Manual review                    ├── Human-in-loop process
                                     └── Bias testing

L3: ADVANCED (H2)                   L4: LEADING (H3+)
├── Automated guardrails             ├── AI audit trail
├── Continuous monitoring            ├── Regulatory compliance
├── AI explainability                ├── AI risk management
├── AI incident response             ├── Third-party AI audit
└── Responsible AI principles        └── Industry advocacy
```
