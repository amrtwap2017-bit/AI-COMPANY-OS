# 12 — Board Reporting

> Board reporting framework for stakeholder communication.

## Reference Chain

| Source | Input |
|--------|-------|
| Phase 10 — Executive-Review.md | Internal reviews |
| Phase 10 — Business-Metrics.md | Business metrics |

## Report Types

| Report | Audience | Frequency | Format | Content |
|--------|----------|-----------|--------|---------|
| Executive summary | Co-founders | Weekly | Slack + doc | Key metrics, blockers, next |
| Monthly board pack | Board/advisors | Monthly | PDF + slides | Metrics, financials, roadmap |
| Quarterly review | Board + team | Quarterly | Presentation | OKRs, strategy, lessons |
| Annual report | All stakeholders | Yearly | PDF | Year in review, next year |
| Investor update | Investors | Monthly | Email | Progress, metrics, asks |

## Board Communication Cadence

| Channel | Content | Frequency |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| Weekly email | Key metrics, updates, blockers | Weekly |
| Board pack | Detailed metrics, analysis | Monthly |
| Board meeting | Presentation, discussion, decisions | Quarterly |
| Investor AMA | Open Q&A | Monthly |
| Annual letter | Year in review, vision | Yearly |
