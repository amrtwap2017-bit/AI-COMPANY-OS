# Continuous Improvement

> The Kaizen engine driving perpetual enterprise evolution.

## Improvement Loop

```
Measure ──► Analyze ──► Prioritize ──► Implement ──► Validate ──► Standardize
   │          │            │              │             │              │
 Current    Root         Based on       Build +      Measure       Update
 state      cause        impact         deploy       results       SOPs
                         + effort
```

## Review Cadence

| Review | Frequency | Participants | Focus |
|--------|-----------|--------------|-------|
| Weekly Triage | Weekly | CTO + COO | New issues, quick wins |
| Monthly Planning | Monthly | Full team | Next month priorities |
| Quarterly Review | Quarterly | Exec Committee | OKR progress |
| Annual Strategy | Annual | All stakeholders | Next year roadmap |

## Kaizen Culture

- Every team member can suggest improvements
- No idea too small
- Improvement suggestions tracked and rewarded
- Postmortems required for all incidents
- Lessons learned shared across the enterprise
