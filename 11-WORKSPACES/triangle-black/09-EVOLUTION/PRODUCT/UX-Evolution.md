## UX Principles

1. **Mobile-first** — Every screen works on mobile
2. **Arabic-first** — RTL is primary, English secondary
3. **Accessibility** — WCAG 2.1 AA minimum
4. **Performance** — Every interaction < 200ms
5. **Consistency** — Single design language
6. **Simplicity** — 3-click rule for all tasks
7. **Feedback** — Every action has visible feedback
