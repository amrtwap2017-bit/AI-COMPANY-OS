## Backlog Structure

```
EPIC: Major initiative spanning multiple sprints
  └── FEATURE: Specific capability
       ├── User Story: From user perspective
       ├── Acceptance Criteria: Done conditions
       └── Technical Tasks: Implementation steps
```

## Priority Rules

| Priority | Definition | Timeline |
|----------|-----------|----------|
| P1 | Critical for business growth | This quarter |
| P2 | Important, strategic value | This half |
| P3 | Valuable but not urgent | This year |
| P4 | Nice to have | Future |

## Backlog Management

| Cadence | Activity | Owner |
|---------|----------|-------|
| Weekly | Triage new items, prioritize | Product |
| Bi-weekly | Sprint planning | Team |
| Monthly | Backlog grooming | CTO + COO |
| Quarterly | Epic prioritization | Exec Committee |
