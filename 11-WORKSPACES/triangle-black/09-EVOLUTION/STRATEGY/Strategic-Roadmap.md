```
├── AI copilots for all domains
├── Data warehouse + BI dashboards

├── Egypt market expansion (30 customers)

H2: SCALE (Months 13-24)
├── Multi-region deployment (GCC)
├── Public API + marketplace
├── Predictive analytics

H3: EXPAND (Months 25-36)
├── Africa expansion
├── Autonomous AI agents
├── IoT integration
├── Industry ecosystem

H4: TRANSFORM (Months 37-60)

└── 3,000+ customers, $3M+ MRR
```
