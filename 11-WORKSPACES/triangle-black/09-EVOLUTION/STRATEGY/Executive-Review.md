| Source | Input |

## Executive Review Cadence

| Review | Frequency | Participants | Duration | Focus |
|--------|-----------|-------------|----------|-------|
| Weekly Strategy Sync | Weekly | CTO + COO | 30 min | Tactical, blockers |
| Monthly Business Review | Monthly | Exec Committee | 60 min | Metrics, revenue, customers |
| Quarterly Strategic Review | Quarterly | All team | 2 hours | OKRs, roadmap, lessons |
| Annual Strategy Offsite | Yearly | Leadership | 1-2 days | 5-year vision, next year plan |
