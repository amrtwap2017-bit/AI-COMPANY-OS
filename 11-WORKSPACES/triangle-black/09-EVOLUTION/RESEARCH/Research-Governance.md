| Source | Input |

## Research Principles

1. **Business-aligned** — Research must tie to business value
2. **Time-boxed** — Research has clear deadlines
3. **Failing fast** — Quick failures are valuable learnings
4. **Share results** — All research shared internally
5. **Protect production** — Sandbox isolation from production
6. **Budget discipline** — Research has clear budget limits

## Research Review Board

| Role | Member | Responsibilities |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| CTO | Technology lead | Technical feasibility |
| COO | Business lead | Business value assessment |
| Innovation Lead | Research lead | Pipeline management |
| Domain Expert | Per-project | Domain validation |

## Research Project Approval

| Criterion | Weight | Threshold |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| Business value | 30% | > 6/10 |
| Technical feasibility | 25% | > 5/10 |
| Strategic alignment | 20% | > 7/10 |
| Resource availability | 15% | Resources available |
| Risk level | 10% | Acceptable risk |

## Research Output Requirements

| Output | Format | Deadline | Audience |
|--------|--------|----------|----------|
| Project brief | Markdown | Start | Team |
| Weekly update | Slack/Trello | Weekly | CTO |
| Final report | Markdown | End | Exec team |
| Demo (if successful) | Video/live | End | All team |
| Product proposal (if applicable) | RFC | After research | Product |

## Intellectual Property

| Aspect | Policy |
|--------|--------|
| Research IP | Owned by Triangle Black |
| Open source contributions | Encouraged for non-core |
| Publication | With CTO approval |
| Patentable inventions | File patent, then publish |
| Third-party IP | Respect licenses, attribution |

## Research Budget Governance

| Threshold | Approval | Tracking |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| $0-500 | CTO | Per-project ledger |
| $500-2,000 | CTO + COO | Per-project + monthly review |
| $2,000+ | Exec team | Full proposal + quarterly review |
