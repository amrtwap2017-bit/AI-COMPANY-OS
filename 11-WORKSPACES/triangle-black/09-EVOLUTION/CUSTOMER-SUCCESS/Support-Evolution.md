## Support Evolution Phases

```
L1: HUMAN-ONLY (V1.0)              L2: AI-ASSISTED (H1)
├── Email + in-app tickets          ├── AI copilot resolves 60%
├── Manual triage                   ├── Human handles complex
├── Knowledge base (manual search)   ├── AI-suggested responses
├── CSAT measured                   ├── Smart routing
└── No SLA tracking                 └── SLA tracking + alerts

L3: AI-FIRST (H2)                   L4: AUTONOMOUS (H3+)
├── AI resolves 80% of tickets      ├── AI resolves 95%+
├── Predictive issue detection       ├── Proactive resolution
├── Self-service knowledge base     ├── Self-healing platform
├── Customer portal                  ├── No-touch support
└── 24/7 AI support                 └── Human only for edge cases
```

## Support Tiers

| Tier | Channel | SLA (First Response) | SLA (Resolution) | Availability |
|------|---------|---------------------|-------------------|--------------|

| Standard | In-app, email | 4 hours | 24 hours | Business hours (8h) |
| Premium | In-app, email, Slack | 1 hour | 4 hours | 24/7 |
| Enterprise | Slack, phone, dedicated | 15 min | 1 hour | 24/7 |
