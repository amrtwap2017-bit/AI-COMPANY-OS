## CS Evolution Phases

```
H1: FOUNDATION (Months 1-12)       H2: SCALE (Months 13-24)
├── CS team structure               ├── Customer health scoring
├── Customer onboarding process     ├── Automated CS workflows
├── NPS program                     ├── Customer community
├── Support ticketing system        ├── Self-service knowledge base
├── Training program               ├── Customer portal
├── Quarterly business reviews     ├── CS platform (Gainsight-like)
└── Customer health framework       └── 100+ customers managed

H3: GROWTH (Months 25-36)          H4: SCALE (Months 37-60)
├── AI-powered CS                  ├── Customer-led growth
├── Predictive churn prevention     ├── Fully automated CS
├── Customer advisory board         ├── 10,000+ customers
├── White-glove onboarding          └── Industry-leading NPS
└── Ecosystem CS (partner success)
```
