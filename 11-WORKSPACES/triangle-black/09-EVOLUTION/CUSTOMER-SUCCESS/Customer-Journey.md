## Journey Stages

```
DISCOVER ──► SIGNUP ──► ONBOARD ──► ADOPT ──► EXPAND ──► ADVOCATE
   │           │          │          │         │           │
Find TB    Create     Setup,    Daily     Upgrade,  Refer,
           account   configure, use       add       case
                     train                modules   study
```

## Stage Details

| Stage | Duration | Activities | Owner | Success Criteria |
|-------|----------|------------|-------|-----------------|
| Discover | 1-30 days | Website, demo, trial | Sales | Demo booked |
| Signup | 1 day | Account creation, contract | Sales | Paid subscription |
| Onboard | 14-30 days | Setup, data import, training | CS + CTO | Hotel live on platform |
| Adopt | 1-3 months | Daily usage, feature adoption | CS | 80% feature adoption |
| Expand | 3-12 months | Upsell, new modules | CS + Sales | Revenue growth |
| Advocate | 12+ months | Referrals, case studies | CS + Marketing | NPS > 50, referral |

## Touchpoints

| Stage | Touchpoints | Channel | Automation |
|-------|-------------|---------|-----------|
| Discover | Website visit, demo, trial | Web, email | Drip campaign |
| Signup | Contract signature, welcome | Email, portal | Auto-welcome |
| Onboard | Setup call, training sessions | Video call, email | Checklist |
| Adopt | Usage report, check-in calls | Email, in-app | Health alerts |
| Expand | Feature announcement, upsell | Email, call | Product suggestions |
| Advocate | NPS survey, referral request | Email, portal | Referral program |

## Journey Metrics

| Stage | Metric | Target |
|-------|--------|--------|
| Discover | Demo → signup rate | > 20% |
| Signup | Signup → onboard start | < 3 days |
| Onboard | Time to first value | < 14 days |
| Adopt | D30 active usage | > 80% |
