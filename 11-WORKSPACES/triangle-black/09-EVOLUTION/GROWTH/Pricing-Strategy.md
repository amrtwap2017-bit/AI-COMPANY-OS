## Pricing Evolution

| Phase | Pricing Model | Monthly Price | Target Customer |
|-------|--------------|---------------|-----------------|
| V1 | Flat per hotel | $100-500 | Small hotels (10-50 rooms) |
| H1 | Tiered per hotel | $100-1,000 | Small-mid hotels |
| H2 | Tiered + per-room | $200-3,000 | Mid-large hotels |
| H3 | Enterprise + usage | $1,000-10,000 | Enterprise hotels, chains |
| H4 | Platform + ecosystem | Custom | All segments |

## Pricing Tiers (H1)

| Tier | Price | Hotels | Features | Target |
|------|-------|--------|----------|--------|
| Starter | $100/mo | 1-20 rooms | Core PMS, reservations, support | Small B&Bs |
| Growth | $300/mo | 20-50 rooms | + housekeeping, maintenance, reports | Mid hotels |
| Professional | $600/mo | 50-100 rooms | + analytics, procurement, inventory | Large hotels |
| Enterprise | Custom | 100+ rooms | + AI copilots, BI, premium support | Chains |

## Pricing Principles

1. **Value-based** — Price based on value delivered
2. **Simple** — Easy to understand, no hidden fees
3. **Scalable** — Pricing grows with customer success
4. **Transparent** — Published pricing, no negotiation
5. **Egypt-market** — Affordable for local market
6. **Annual discount** — 15% discount for annual billing
