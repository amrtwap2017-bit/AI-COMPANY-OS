## Sales Evolution Phases

```
L1: FOUNDER-LED (V1-H1)             L2: SALES TEAM (H2)
├── CTO/COO sells                    ├── 2 SDRs + 1 AE
├── Demo + close                     ├── Outbound prospecting
├── Warm leads only                  ├── CRM-driven pipeline
├── Manual proposals                 ├── Proposal templates
└── No CRM                           └── Sales playbook

L3: SCALED (H3)                     L4: ENTERPRISE (H4)
├── 5+ sales team                   ├── Enterprise sales team
├── Channel sales                   ├── Strategic account management
├── Inside sales                    ├── Multi-region sales
├── Sales ops function              ├── Partner-driven sales
└── Revenue operations             └── Sales engineering
```

## Sales Process

```
Lead ──► Qualify ──► Demo ──► Proposal ──► Close ──► Handoff to CS
  │        │         │         │            │          │
 Inbound  BANT     Product   Quote +     Contract   Onboarding
 or      budget,  demo     SOW        signed     kickoff
 outbound authority,
          need,
          timeline
```

## Sales Metrics

| Metric | H1 Target | H2 Target | H3 Target |
|--------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Demo → close rate | 25% | 30% | 35% |
| Average deal size | $500/mo | $1,000/mo | $3,000/mo |
| Sales cycle (days) | 14 | 21 | 30 |
| Pipeline coverage | 3x | 4x | 3x |
| Customer acquisition cost | $500 | $1,000 | $2,000 |
| LTV:CAC ratio | 5:1 | 4:1 | 5:1 |
