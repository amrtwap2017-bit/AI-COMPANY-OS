## Growth Phases

```
H1: VALIDATE (Months 1-12)          H2: SCALE (Months 13-24)
├── 30 customers (Egypt)            ├── 300 customers (Egypt + GCC)
├── $50K MRR                        ├── $300K MRR
├── Product-led growth (organic)    ├── Sales team (2 people)
├── Founder-led sales               ├── Channel partners
├── Content marketing               ├── Paid acquisition
├── NPS > 40                        ├── NPS > 50
├── Churn < 5%                      └── Churn < 3%
└── Negative net dollar churn

H3: EXPAND (Months 25-36)           H4: DOMINATE (Months 37-60)
├── 1,000 customers (MENA)          ├── 3,000+ customers (Global)
├── $1M MRR                         ├── $3M+ MRR
├── Enterprise sales team           ├── Industry leadership
├── Partnership ecosystem           ├── Ecosystem-driven growth
├── International offices           ├── Global brand
├── NPS > 60                        ├── NPS > 70
└── Churn < 2%                      └── Churn < 1%
```
