## Marketing Evolution Phases

```
L1: CONTENT (H1)                    L2: DEMAND GEN (H2)
├── Blog (weekly)                    ├── Paid ads (Google, LinkedIn)
├── SEO optimization                 ├── Webinars
├── Social media (LinkedIn)          ├── Content syndication
├── Case studies                     ├── Email nurture campaigns
├── Email newsletter                 ├── Retargeting
└── Community building               └── Landing page optimization

L3: FULL FUNNEL (H3)                L4: BRAND (H4)
├── Multi-channel campaigns          ├── Brand awareness
├── ABM (account-based marketing)    ├── Industry events
├── Marketing automation             ├── Thought leadership
├── Partner marketing               ├── PR and media
└── International marketing          └── Awards and recognition
```
