## Hiring Principles

1. **Culture first** — Hire for values, train for skills
2. **Remote-friendly** — Egypt-first, remote-capable
3. **Diversity** — Diverse backgrounds, perspectives
4. **Growth mindset** — Continuous learners
5. **Customer empathy** — Understand hospitality
