# Sales Process Checklist

## Lead Stage
- [ ] Lead captured in CRM with complete contact information
- [ ] Lead source identified and recorded
- [ ] Duplicate check performed
- [ ] Lead assigned to Sales Representative
- [ ] Initial contact attempted within 2 hours
- [ ] BANT qualification completed
- [ ] Lead scored (Hot / Warm / Cold)
- [ ] Cold lead moved to nurture sequence

## Opportunity Stage
- [ ] Opportunity created in CRM with value and close date
- [ ] Key stakeholders identified and mapped
- [ ] Client requirements documented
- [ ] Competitive landscape understood
- [ ] Budget confirmed (or range established)
- [ ] Decision-making process understood
- [ ] Timeline confirmed
- [ ] Follow-up schedule established in CRM

## Proposal Stage
- [ ] Proposal drafted using approved template
- [ ] Scope of work defined and reviewed
- [ ] Pricing calculated within approved margin range
- [ ] Operations Director reviewed scope and pricing
- [ ] Sales Manager approved commercial terms
- [ ] Proposal branded correctly
- [ ] Proposal delivered to client (portal / in-person)
- [ ] Proposal logged in CRM with version

## Negotiation Stage
- [ ] Client feedback received and logged
- [ ] Price concessions within approved discount band
- [ ] Terms negotiated (payment, timeline, SLA)
- [ ] Legal review completed (if required)
- [ ] All changes documented and approved

## Closing Stage
- [ ] Contract signed by all parties
- [ ] Signed contract filed in CRM and document vault
- [ ] Payment terms activated in finance system
- [ ] Kickoff meeting scheduled with operations team
- [ ] Handover document completed
- [ ] Sales-to-Operations handover meeting held
- [ ] Welcome email sent to client
- [ ] Client added to platform and portal activated
- [ ] CRM opportunity marked Closed Won

## Post-Close
- [ ] Client success contact assigned
- [ ] 30-day check-in scheduled
- [ ] Onboarding process initiated
- [ ] Reference case prepared (if applicable)
