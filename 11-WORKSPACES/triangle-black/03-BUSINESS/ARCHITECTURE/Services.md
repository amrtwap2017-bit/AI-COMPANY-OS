# Services Catalog

## Service Architecture

Triangle Black services are structured in three tiers, each building on the previous:

```
Tier 1 — Foundation (keep the hotel running)
Tier 2 — Optimization (run better, cost less)
Tier 3 — Transformation (future-proof the asset)
```

---

## Tier 1: Foundation

### 1.1 Operational Partnership

**What it is:** An ongoing managed service agreement where Triangle Black acts as the hotel's external engineering operations department.

**Inclusions:**
- Dedicated account manager and technical liaison
- Preventive maintenance scheduling and tracking
- Vendor and contractor coordination
- Emergency response protocol management
- Monthly performance reporting
- Quarterly business reviews

**Delivery:** On-site presence as agreed per tier, backed by Triangle Black operations center.

---

### 1.2 Engineering Procurement

**What it is:** End-to-end procurement of all engineering supplies — from consumables to capital equipment.

**Inclusions:**
- Sourcing and vendor qualification
- Price negotiation and benchmarking
- Order processing and tracking
- Quality inspection on delivery
- Inventory management for frequently used items
- Warranty claim management

**Delivery:** Digital procurement platform + physical delivery to hotel loading bay.

---

### 1.3 Emergency Response

**What it is:** Guaranteed rapid response for critical engineering breakdowns.

**Inclusions:**
- 24/7 emergency call line
- 2-hour/4-hour/8-hour response tiers
- Diagnostic and repair service
- Temporary hotfix capability while permanent solution is procured
- Incident report with root cause analysis

**Delivery:** On-site by Triangle Black technician or vetted partner.

---

## Tier 2: Optimization

### 2.1 Preventive & Predictive Maintenance

**What it is:** Scheduled maintenance programs that extend equipment life and reduce unplanned downtime.

**Inclusions:**
- Equipment inventory and condition assessment
- Manufacturer-recommended maintenance schedules
- Monthly/quarterly/annual maintenance execution
- Spare parts forecasting and staging
- Performance benchmarking year-over-year

**Delivery:** Scheduled on-site visits per maintenance calendar.

---

### 2.2 Vendor & Contractor Management

**What it is:** Triangle Black manages the entire vendor ecosystem on behalf of the hotel.

**Inclusions:**
- Vendor qualification and due diligence
- Contract negotiation and management
- Scheduling and dispatch coordination
- Quality and compliance verification
- Performance scorecarding
- Invoice validation and processing support

**Delivery:** Centralized via Triangle Black operations team.

---

### 2.3 Compliance & Safety Management

**What it is:** Ensuring the hotel meets all regulatory, safety, and insurance requirements for engineering operations.

**Inclusions:**
- Regulatory compliance audit (civil defense, licensing, environmental)
- Fire safety system inspection and certification support
- Lifting equipment inspection and certification
- Electrical safety verification
- Documentation management for audit readiness
- Compliance calendar and deadline tracking

**Delivery:** Annual audit plus quarterly check-ins.

---

## Tier 3: Transformation

### 3.1 Engineering Design & Consulting

**What it is:** Professional engineering design services for renovations, expansions, and system upgrades.

**Inclusions:**
- MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing) design
- HVAC load calculations and system design
- Electrical distribution and backup power design
- Fire safety engineering
- Sustainability and energy efficiency consulting
- Technical specifications and tender documentation

**Delivery:** Design package delivered per project scope.

---

### 3.2 Project Management

**What it is:** Full lifecycle project management for capital improvement projects.

**Inclusions:**
- Project scoping and budgeting
- Contractor tender and selection
- Construction supervision
- Quality control and progress monitoring
- Commissioning and handover
- As-built documentation

**Delivery:** On-site project manager for duration of project.

---

### 3.3 Energy & Sustainability

**What it is:** Programs to reduce energy consumption, water usage, and environmental footprint.

**Inclusions:**
- Energy audit (electricity, gas, water)
- Energy conservation measure identification
- ROI analysis for capital upgrades
- Solar / renewable energy feasibility studies
- Waste management system design
- Green certification support (LEED, Green Star, etc.)

**Delivery:** Audit report plus implementation roadmap.

---

### 3.4 Digital Operations Platform

**What it is:** Technology layer that connects all Triangle Black services into a single dashboard.

**Inclusions:**
- Work order management (request, assign, track, close)
- Asset register with maintenance history
- Procurement tracking (order → delivery → invoice)
- Compliance calendar with automated reminders
- Performance dashboards for hotel management
- Mobile access for engineering team

**Delivery:** SaaS platform access included with Operational Partnership.
