# Stakeholder Map

## Stakeholder Categories

Triangle Black operates within a complex ecosystem of stakeholders. Understanding their interests, influence, and engagement strategy is critical to success.

---

## Internal Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Interest | Influence | Engagement Strategy |
|-------------|----------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Founders / Leadership** | Business growth, profitability, reputation | High | Weekly strategy, monthly board review |
| **Engineering Team** | Technical quality, career growth, tools | High | Daily stand-ups, quarterly training, tech stack input |
| **Sales & BD** | Commission, sales tools, pipeline quality | High | Weekly pipeline review, collateral support |
| **Operations / Delivery** | Resource planning, SLA achievement, client satisfaction | High | Monthly ops review, client feedback loops |
| **Finance & Admin** | Cash flow, cost control, compliance | Medium | Monthly financial review, audit support |
| **Support Staff** | Clear processes, fair treatment, safety | Low-Medium | Quarterly all-hands, open door policy |

## Stakeholder Power-Interest Grid

```
High Power         |  Hotel Owners         |  Chief Engineers
Low Interest  ------|-----------------------|---------------------- High Interest
                   |  Regulators           |  General Managers
                   |  Insurance Companies  |  Financial Controllers
                   |                       |  Purchasing Managers
Low Power          |  Technology Vendors   |  Triangle Black Team
                   |  Equipment MFGs       |  Vetted Contractors
                   |  Hospitality Assoc.   |  Suppliers
```

**Key to manage closely (High Power, High Interest):** Hotel Owners, General Managers, Chief Engineers, Financial Controllers

**Key to keep satisfied (High Power, Low Interest):** Regulators, Insurance Companies

**Key to keep informed (Low Power, High Interest):** Suppliers, Contractors, Triangle Black Team

**Key to monitor (Low Power, Low Interest):** Technology Vendors, Hospitality Associations
