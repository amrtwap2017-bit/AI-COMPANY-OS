# Pricing Strategy

## Pricing Philosophy

Triangle Black uses **value-based pricing** anchored to the cost of the problem being solved. Hotels spend 12-18% of EBITDA on unplanned engineering costs. Our pricing is positioned at a fraction of that — we win when the hotel saves money.

**Core principle:** The hotel pays less for Triangle Black than they currently lose to engineering inefficiency.

---

## Pricing by Service

### 1. Operational Partnership Fee

**Model:** Fixed monthly retainer, tiered by hotel size and scope

| Hotel Size | Basic (EGP/mo) | Standard (EGP/mo) | Premium (EGP/mo) |
|------------|---------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| < 100 rooms | 25,000 - 35,000 | 35,000 - 50,000 | 50,000 - 70,000 |
| 100 - 250 rooms | 40,000 - 60,000 | 60,000 - 90,000 | 90,000 - 130,000 |
| 250+ rooms | 55,000 - 80,000 | 80,000 - 120,000 | 120,000 - 180,000 |

**Pricing rationale:** Based on 5-8% of the hotel's annual engineering opex, replacing 20-30% of that opex in avoided waste and savings.

**Discount structure for multi-property:**
- 2-3 hotels: 5% discount per property
- 4-6 hotels: 10% discount per property
- 7+ hotels: 15% discount per property

---

### 2. Engineering Supplies

**Model:** Cost-plus markup

| Category | Markup |
|----------|--------|
| Consumables | 18-20% |
| Specialized equipment | 15-18% |
| Safety & compliance items | 20-22% |
| Emergency rush orders | 22-25% |

**Pricing rationale:** Our cost + transparent markup. Hotels see the actual supplier invoice. Compare to current hotel procurement where markup is opaque and typically 25-40%.

**Minimum order value:** EGP 2,000 per transaction. Below this, handling fee of EGP 200 applies.

---

### 3. Contracting Services

**Model:** Fixed-price quotation per project

| Project Type | Typical Fee Range | Day Rate (T&M) |
|-------------|------------------|----------------|
| HVAC | EGP 200,000 - 800,000 | EGP 3,500 - 5,000 |
| Electrical | EGP 150,000 - 500,000 | EGP 3,000 - 4,500 |
| Plumbing | EGP 100,000 - 400,000 | EGP 2,500 - 4,000 |
| Fire safety | EGP 180,000 - 600,000 | EGP 3,500 - 5,000 |
| BMS / Controls | EGP 250,000 - 1,000,000 | EGP 4,000 - 6,000 |

**Pricing rationale:** Fixed-price for defined scope; time-and-materials for variable scope. All quotes include 10% contingency allowance disclosed to client.

---

### 4. Design Services

**Model:** Fixed fee based on project complexity

| Service | Fee Range | Payment Milestones |
|---------|-----------|-------------------|
| Engineering systems audit | EGP 30,000 - 60,000 | 50% start / 50% on delivery |
| MEP design for renovation | EGP 50,000 - 150,000 | 30%/40%/30% |
| Compliance gap analysis | EGP 20,000 - 40,000 | 50% / 50% |
| Energy audit | EGP 40,000 - 80,000 | 50% / 50% |

**Pricing rationale:** Based on engineering hours (EGP 500-800/hour blended rate) + 20-30% for methodology/IP.

---

### 5. Project Management

**Model:** Percentage of total project value

| Project Value | Fee % |
|--------------|-------|
| Up to EGP 500,000 | 10-12% |
| EGP 500,000 - 2,000,000 | 8-10% |
| Above EGP 2,000,000 | 6-8% |

**Pricing rationale:** Industry standard for hospitality PM in Egypt is 8-12%. We anchor at market rate and differentiate on transparency and reporting.

---

### 6. Emergency Response

**Model:** Retainer clients — premium per call-out based on response tier. Non-retainer clients — higher fee.

| Response Tier | Retainer Client | Non-Retainer Client |
|--------------|----------------|-------------------|
| 2-hour | EGP 3,000 - 5,000 | EGP 6,000 - 10,000 |
| 4-hour | EGP 1,500 - 3,000 | EGP 3,000 - 6,000 |
| 8-hour | Included | EGP 2,000 - 4,000 |

**Pricing rationale:** Emergency response is a premium service. Retainer clients get priority pricing as part of the partnership model.

## Payment Terms

| Client Type | Terms |
|------------|-------|
| Retainer clients | Net-15 from invoice date |
| Project clients | 30% mobilization / 40% milestone / 30% on completion |
| Supplies | Net-30 for retainer clients, Net-15 for others |
| Emergency | Payment on completion (credit card or bank transfer) |

**Late payment:** 2% monthly interest on overdue amounts. Service suspension after 60 days overdue.
