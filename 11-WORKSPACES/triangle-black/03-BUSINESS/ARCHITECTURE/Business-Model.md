# Business Model

## Value Proposition

Triangle Black is the **Operational Engineering Partner** for hotels in Egypt. We eliminate the fragmentation between engineering procurement, maintenance operations, and compliance management by providing a single-source platform backed by boots-on-the-ground execution.

Hotels in Egypt lose 12-18% of annual EBITDA to unplanned maintenance, emergency procurement at inflated prices, and compliance penalties. Triangle Black reduces this by delivering integrated engineering operations at a predictable monthly cost.

## Business Model Canvas

### 1. Customer Segments

- **Primary:** Mid-scale to upper-upscale hotels in Sharm El Sheikh (60-300+ rooms)
- **Secondary:** Hotel management companies operating multiple properties in Egypt
- **Tertiary:** New hotel developments in the Egypt pipeline (143 hotels / 33,926 rooms)
- **Future:** Hospitality groups across MENA region

### 2. Value Propositions

| Segment | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Hotel Owners | Higher asset value, reduced opex, compliance assurance |
| General Managers | Single point of accountability for all engineering ops |
| Chief Engineers | Access to supply chain, technical support, documented processes |
| Purchasing Managers | 15-25% reduction in procurement costs via consolidated buying |
| Financial Controllers | Predictable monthly costs vs. unpredictable emergency spend |

### 3. Channels

- **Direct Sales:** Executive-level business development targeting owners and GMs
- **Industry Events:** Egypt Hotel Association, Arabian Travel Market, HACE
- **Referral Network:** Existing supplier and contractor partners
- **Digital Presence:** Website, LinkedIn, hospitality industry publications
- **Strategic Alliances:** Hotel management companies, development consultants

### 4. Customer Relationships

- **Dedicated Account Manager** per hotel relationship
- **Quarterly Business Reviews** with performance dashboards
- **24/7 Emergency Support** line for critical breakdowns
- **Annual Operations Audit** with improvement roadmap
- **Client Advisory Board** (target: formed at 10 clients)

### 5. Revenue Streams

| Stream | Type | Target Margin |
|--------|------|---------------|
| Operational Partnership Fee | Monthly retainer | 25-35% |
| Engineering Supplies | Markup on procurement | 18-22% |
| Contracting Services | Project-based | 20-28% |
| Design Services | Fixed-fee per project | 35-45% |
| Project Management | % of project value | 30-40% |
| Emergency Response | Premium fee per call-out | 20-25% |

*Detailed breakdown in Revenue-Architecture.md*

### 6. Key Resources

- **Supply Chain Network:** Vetted suppliers across HVAC, electrical, plumbing, fire safety, BMS
- **Technical Team:** Licensed engineers, technicians, project managers
- **Digital Platform:** Procurement, asset management, and work order system
- **Inventory Hub:** Strategic stock of high-failure-rate parts in Sharm El Sheikh
- **Reputation:** Track record with first anchor clients in Sharm

### 7. Key Activities

- Procurement and supply chain management
- Preventive and reactive maintenance execution
- Engineering project management and delivery
- Compliance and safety auditing
- Vendor qualification and performance management
- Client reporting and analytics

### 8. Key Partnerships

- **Equipment Manufacturers:** Direct supply agreements (pricing and priority)
- **Local Contractors:** Pool of vetted execution partners for overflow work
- **Technology Vendors:** Building management systems, IoT, procurement platforms
- **Training Institutes:** Pipeline of qualified technicians
- **Hotel Associations:** Market access and credibility

## Value Delivery Model

```
    Hotel Need → Assessment → Solution Design → 
    Procurement + Execution → Performance Monitoring → 
    Reporting & Continuous Improvement
```

Triangle Black sits between the hotel operator and the fragmented vendor ecosystem, consolidating dozens of supplier relationships into one managed partnership.

## Competitive Advantages

1. **Physical presence in Sharm El Sheikh** — not a Cairo-based company servicing Sharm remotely
2. **Hotel-specific engineering expertise** — hospitality is a niche, not a sideline
3. **Procurement leverage** — consolidated buying across multiple properties
4. **Outcome-based pricing** — clients pay for results, not headcount
5. **Digital-native operations** — all processes tracked, measured, reported
