# Revenue Architecture

## Revenue Stream Overview

| Stream | % of Total Revenue (Target) | Margin Target | Type | Recurring? |
|--------|----------------------------|---------------|------|------------|
| Operational Partnership Fee | 40-50% | 25-35% | Monthly retainer | Yes |
| Engineering Supplies | 20-25% | 18-22% | Markup on procured goods | Yes |
| Contracting Services | 15-20% | 20-28% | Project-based | No |
| Design Services | 5-10% | 35-45% | Fixed fee per project | No |
| Project Management | 5-8% | 30-40% | % of project value | No |
| Emergency Response | 3-5% | 20-25% | Per call-out premium | Occasional |

## 1. Operational Partnership Fee

**Model:** Monthly retainer based on hotel size and scope of engagement

| Hotel Size | Basic | Standard | Premium |
|------------|-------|----------|---------|
| < 100 rooms | EGP 25,000-35,000 | EGP 35,000-50,000 | EGP 50,000-70,000 |
| 100-250 rooms | EGP 40,000-60,000 | EGP 60,000-90,000 | EGP 90,000-130,000 |
| 250+ rooms | EGP 55,000-80,000 | EGP 80,000-120,000 | EGP 120,000-180,000 |

**Scope tiers:**
- **Basic:** Procurement support + quarterly audits + emergency response backup
- **Standard:** Basic + preventive maintenance planning + vendor management + monthly reporting
- **Premium:** Standard + on-site engineering liaison + inventory management + 24/7 dedicated support

**Target Margin: 25-35%**

## 2. Engineering Supplies

**Model:** Cost + markup on all procured items through Triangle Black supply chain

| Category | Markup | Market Benchmark |
|----------|--------|------------------|
| High-volume consumables (filters, belts, gaskets) | 18-20% | Hotels pay 25-40% retail |
| Specialized equipment (compressors, pumps, chillers) | 15-18% | Hotels pay 20-35% retail |
| Safety and compliance items | 20-22% | Hotels pay 25-30% retail |
| Emergency rush orders | 22-25% | Hotels pay 40-60% from local vendors |

**Target Margin: 18-22%** (lower than retail because volume and prepayment terms)

**Key metric:** Monthly procurement volume per hotel. Target: EGP 50,000-200,000/month per hotel depending on size and season.

## 3. Contracting Services

**Model:** Fixed-price or time-and-materials for engineering projects

| Project Type | Typical Value | Duration | Margin Target |
|-------------|---------------|----------|---------------|
| HVAC overhaul | EGP 200,000-800,000 | 1-4 weeks | 22-28% |
| Electrical system upgrade | EGP 150,000-500,000 | 1-3 weeks | 20-26% |
| Plumbing renovation | EGP 100,000-400,000 | 1-2 weeks | 20-25% |
| Fire safety system install | EGP 180,000-600,000 | 2-4 weeks | 24-28% |
| BMS installation/upgrade | EGP 250,000-1,000,000 | 2-8 weeks | 25-30% |

**Target Margin: 20-28%**

## 4. Design Services

**Model:** Fixed fee per engineering design project

| Service | Fee Range | Margin Target |
|---------|-----------|---------------|
| Engineering systems audit | EGP 30,000-60,000 | 40-45% |
| MEP design for renovation | EGP 50,000-150,000 | 35-40% |
| Compliance gap analysis | EGP 20,000-40,000 | 40-45% |
| Sustainability/energy audit | EGP 40,000-80,000 | 35-40% |

**Target Margin: 35-45%** — high margin, low cost of delivery (engineering hours only)

## 5. Project Management

**Model:** Percentage of total project value

| Project Value | Fee % | Fee Range |
|--------------|-------|-----------|
| Up to EGP 500,000 | 10-12% | EGP 50,000-60,000 |
| EGP 500,000-2,000,000 | 8-10% | EGP 40,000-200,000 |
| Above EGP 2,000,000 | 6-8% | Negotiable |

**Target Margin: 30-40%**

## 6. Emergency Response

**Model:** Premium fee on top of standard margin for urgent call-outs

| Response Time | Premium Fee | Typical Total per Call-out |
|--------------|-------------|---------------------------|
| Within 2 hours | EGP 3,000-5,000 | EGP 8,000-20,000 |
| Within 4 hours | EGP 1,500-3,000 | EGP 5,000-12,000 |
| Within 8 hours | EGP 0 (included in retainer) | Standard rates |

**Target Margin: 20-25%** — higher COGS due to overtime/expedited logistics
