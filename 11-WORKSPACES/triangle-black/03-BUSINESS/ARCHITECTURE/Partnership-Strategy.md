# Partnership Strategy

## Partnership Philosophy

Partnerships are a **force multiplier** for Triangle Black. We do not need to own trucks, employ every technician, or build every piece of software. Instead, we build a carefully curated ecosystem of partners who extend our reach, capacity, and capability.

**Core principle:** Partners should make more money with us than without us.

---

## Partner Categories

### 1. Supply Partners

#### Profile
Equipment manufacturers, authorized distributors, and wholesale suppliers.

#### Key Partners to Recruit

| Category | Target Partners | Priority |
|----------|----------------|----------|
| HVAC equipment | Carrier, Trane, LG, Daikin, Gree, Zamil | High |
| Electrical | Schneider Electric, ABB, Siemens, Elsewedy | High |
| Plumbing | Grohe, American Standard, Roca, local suppliers | High |
| Fire safety | Honeywell, Bosch, Siemens, Halma, local suppliers | Medium |
| BMS / Controls | Honeywell, Siemens, Johnson Controls, Schneider | Medium |
| Spare parts | Specialized HVAC/electrical parts distributors | High |
| Pool & water treatment | Pentair, Hayward, local chemical suppliers | Medium |
| Kitchen equipment | Rational, Hobart, Alto-Shaam, Electrolux | Low-Medium |

#### Partnership Model

| Element | Terms |
|---------|-------|
| Pricing | Preferential distributor pricing (15-25% below list) |
| Payment terms | Net-30 to Net-60 (aligned with client payment collection) |
| Stock commitment | Supplier holds buffer stock for Triangle Black clients |
| Exclusivity | Non-exclusive (we want multi-source flexibility) |
| Warranty | Direct manufacturer warranty + Triangle Black handling |
| Returns | 30-day return window for unused stock |

#### Value Exchange

**What the partner gets:**
- Regular, predictable volume from multiple hotels
- Single point of contact (vs. dealing with each hotel individually)
- Faster payment (Triangle Black pays on terms; hotels often delay)
- Technical specification inclusion in our recommendations
- Access to new hotel clients through our relationships

**What Triangle Black gets:**
- Better pricing than any single hotel can negotiate
- Priority allocation during supply shortages
- Technical support and training for our team
- Warranty support and easy returns
- Credit terms that help our cash flow

### 2. Contractor Partners

#### Profile
Vetted MEP contractors and specialized tradespeople who execute installation and repair work.

#### Partner Qualification Criteria

| Criterion | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Licensing | Valid commercial registration and trade license |
| Insurance | Public liability insurance (min EGP 2M coverage) |
| Experience | Minimum 5 years in hospitality or commercial MEP |
| References | Minimum 3 client references (hotel preferred) |
| Team size | Minimum 5 permanent technicians |
| Safety record | No serious incidents in past 3 years |
| Financial stability | No bankruptcy or major legal disputes |
| Geographic | Based in or near target market |

#### Partnership Tiers

| Tier | Qualification | Benefits | Number of Partners |
|------|--------------|----------|-------------------|
| **Preferred** | Meets all criteria + 3 years partnership | Priority work allocation, faster payment terms (Net-15), training access, brand co-marketing | 3-5 per market |
| **Approved** | Meets all criteria | Regular work allocation, Net-30 terms, quality inspections | 5-10 per market |
| **Registered** | Meets minimum criteria | Occasional work allocation, Net-45 terms, annual review | 10-20 per market |

#### Contractor Agreement Terms

| Element | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Pricing | Fixed rate card for standard items; project quotes for custom work |
| Payment | Net-30 to Net-15 (Preferred tier), upon client payment to Triangle Black |
| SLA | Response time commitments, quality standards, warranty on work |
| Quality | Right to inspect; rework at contractor cost if below standard |
| Exclusivity | Non-exclusive (contractors can work with other clients) |
| Termination | 30-day notice from either party |

### 3. Technology Partners

#### Profile
Software and hardware partners who provide the technology layer for our services.

| Partner Type | Examples | Role |
|-------------|----------|------|
| Procurement platform | Tradogram, Procurify, Zapro | Digital PO and invoice management |
| Asset management | Maintenance Connection, Limble, UpKeep | Work order and asset tracking |
| BMS / IoT | Siemens, Honeywell, Johnson Controls | Building monitoring and automation |
| Communication | Slack, Teams, WhatsApp Business | Client and team communication |
| Accounting | Zoho Books, Odoo, QuickBooks | Financial management |
| Document management | Google Workspace, SharePoint | Documentation and compliance records |

#### Partnership Approach

- **Phase 1 (Year 1):** Off-the-shelf SaaS tools integrated via API or manual processes. No custom development.
- **Phase 2 (Year 2-3):** Custom-built integrated platform (includes procurement, asset management, client dashboards, compliance tracking). Build vs. buy decision at this stage.
- **Phase 3 (Year 4-5):** Platform licensed to partners in other markets. Technology becomes a revenue stream.

### 4. Strategic Partners

#### Profile
Organizations that provide market access, credibility, or complementary services.

| Partner Type | Examples | Value to Triangle Black | Value to Partner |
|-------------|----------|------------------------|------------------|
| Hotel Associations | Egypt Hotel Association, HACE | Credibility, member access, industry intel | Member service enhancement |
| Management Companies | Local hotel operators | Multi-property deals, centralized relationships | Better engineering support for properties |
| Training Institutes | Technical colleges, vocational schools | Talent pipeline, training partnerships | Graduate placement, curriculum input |
| Insurance Companies | Egyptian insurers | Preferred provider status, client referrals | Reduced claims through better maintenance |
| Real Estate Developers | Hospitality developers | Pre-opening contracts, specification influence | Engineering design and operational readiness |
| International Brands | Marriott, Hilton, Accor (local offices) | Approved vendor status, chain property access | Local engineering execution partner |

#### Strategic Partnership Development Process

1. **Identify** — target organizations with aligned interests
2. **Approach** — warm introduction through network
3. **Propose** — clear value exchange (not one-sided)
4. **Pilot** — small collaboration to prove concept
5. **Formalize** — MOU or agreement with defined terms
6. **Review** — annual partnership review and renewal

## Partner Code of Conduct

All Triangle Black partners agree to:

1. **Quality standards** — work meets agreed specifications and quality standards
2. **Safety first** — all work follows safety protocols; no shortcuts
3. **Transparency** — honest pricing, no hidden fees, no kickbacks
4. **Professionalism** — respectful behavior toward clients, team, and end users
5. **Confidentiality** — protect client and Triangle Black proprietary information
6. **Continuous improvement** — participate in feedback and training programs

Violation of the Code of Conduct may result in downgrade or termination of partnership status.
