# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Objective

Acquire 10 operational partnership clients in Sharm El Sheikh within 12 months, establishing Triangle Black as the proven engineering operations partner for Egyptian hospitality.

## Phase 1: Pre-Revenue (Months 1-3)

### 1.1 Anchor Client Recruitment

**Target profile:** 200-400 room hotel in Sharm El Sheikh with:
- Existing pain around engineering procurement and maintenance
- Owner or GM open to outsourced operational support
- Potential as a case study / reference account

**Offer:** First 2 clients get:
- 3-month pilot at 50% of standard retainer
- No long-term lock-in (month-to-month for pilot period)
- Full scope of services (Premium tier)
- Dedicated on-site engineering liaison

**Conversion target:** 2 anchor clients signed by end of Month 3.

### 1.2 Credibility Building

- Register with Egypt Hotel Association (EHA)
- Secure professional liability insurance
- Develop services brochure in English and Arabic
- Create case study template ready to populate after pilot clients
- Set up LinkedIn presence with hospitality engineering thought leadership

### 1.3 Supply Chain Activation

- Sign preferential pricing agreements with 10 key suppliers
- Establish inventory hub in Sharm El Sheikh
- Onboard 5 vetted contractor partners
- Implement procurement and work order platform

---

## Phase 2: Early Traction (Months 4-8)

### 2.1 Sales Channels

| Channel | Priority | Expected Conversion |
|---------|----------|-------------------|
| Direct outreach to hotel GMs and owners | High | 1 in 10 meetings → client |
| Referrals from anchor clients | High | 1 in 5 referrals → client |
| Hotel management company relationships | Medium | 1 in 8 intro → client |
| Industry events and networking | Medium | 1 in 20 contacts → client |
| Digital inbound (LinkedIn, website) | Low | Support role only |

### 2.2 Target List — Sharm El Sheikh (Priority Order)

1. Hotels where we have an existing contact or introduction path
2. Hotels with recent negative reviews around maintenance/engineering
3. Independent hotels (not part of large chains with centralized procurement)
4. Hotels undergoing renovation or expansion
5. Hotels with expatriate GMs who value operational standards

### 2.3 Sales Process

```
Identify → Research → Warm Introduction → 
Discovery Meeting → Site Assessment → 
Proposal → Negotiation → Onboarding
```

**Timeline per deal:** 4-8 weeks from first contact to signed agreement.

### 2.4 Conviction Tools

- **ROI Calculator:** Show hotel their estimated annual savings
- **Site Assessment Report:** Free 1-day engineering walkthrough (delivers immediate value)
- **Reference Calls:** Arrange with pilot clients
- **Service Level Guarantee:** Written SLA with remedies for missed targets

---

## Phase 3: Scale (Months 9-12)

### 3.1 Target: 10 Clients

- 2 anchor clients (signed Months 1-3)
- 4 additional clients (Months 4-8)
- 4 further clients (Months 9-12)

### 3.2 Referral Program

- Existing clients receive 1 month free retainer for successful referral
- Introduce Triangle Black to 3 other hotel GMs → receive 2 weeks free

### 3.3 Case Study Development

Publish results from pilot clients:
- % reduction in emergency call-outs
- % savings on engineering procurement
- Improvement in compliance scores
- Guest satisfaction impact (maintenance-related reviews)

### 3.4 Paid Marketing (from Month 9)

- LinkedIn sponsored content targeting hospitality decision-makers
- Google Ads for "hotel engineering Egypt" and related terms
- Sponsorship of 1 hospitality industry event in Sharm or Cairo
