# Ubiquitous Language

A complete glossary of domain terms used consistently across all bounded contexts in the Triangle Black Digital Operations Ecosystem.

## A–C

| Term | Definition | Context |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Account | A client organization entity within the CRM system | CRM |
| Activity | A scheduled or completed action item associated with a client interaction | CRM |
| Assessment | Technical evaluation of a hotel's engineering systems to determine scope of work | Engineering Assessment |
| Bid Bond | A guarantee submitted with a proposal to ensure the bidder will honor their offer | Quotation |
| BoQ | Bill of Quantities — itemized list of materials, labor, and equipment for a project | Project |
| Bounded Context | A logical boundary within which a particular domain model applies | All |
| Business Event | A real-world occurrence that triggers one or more domain workflows | All |
| Campaign | A marketing initiative targeting a specific segment or audience | Marketing |
| Change Order | A formal document authorizing a change to the original project scope, budget, or timeline | Project |
| Client | An individual representing a client organization; may serve as primary contact | CRM |
| Client Portal | The web-based interface through which clients interact with the ecosystem | ClientPortal |
| Commercial Term | Pricing, payment schedule, and financial conditions of a contract | Contract |
| Condition Report | A document capturing the physical state of hotel assets and facilities | Site Survey |
| Context Map | A diagram showing relationships and integrations between bounded contexts | All |

## D–F

| Term | Definition | Context |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Dashboard | Aggregated view of KPIs and operational metrics | Dashboard |
| Defect | A non-conformance or quality issue identified during QA/QC | QA/QC |
| Delivery Note | A document signed upon receipt of goods at site | Procurement / Supply |
| Document | Any file or record managed within the document system | Document |
| Domain Event | A fact that has occurred within the system, emitted for cross-context communication | All |
| Entity | A domain object with a distinct identity that persists over time | All |
| Equipment Register | A log of all equipment assigned to or installed on a project | Inventory / Project |
| Estimate | A preliminary calculation of the cost and effort for a scope of work | Quotation |
| Evaluation Matrix | A scoring framework used to assess vendor proposals | Vendor Selection |
| Follow-up | A planned future interaction with a lead or client | CRM |
| Framework Agreement | A long-term agreement with a vendor establishing terms for future purchases | Procurement |

## G–L

| Term | Definition | Context |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Handover | The formal transfer of a completed project to the client | Handover |
| Inspection | A physical check of materials, workmanship, or systems against specifications | QA/QC |
| Inventory Item | A tracked unit of material or equipment in stock or on order | Inventory |
| Invoice | A billing document issued to a client for services rendered | Contract / Finance |
| Issue | A problem or risk logged during project execution | Project |
| KPI | Key Performance Indicator used to measure operational success | All |
| Lead | A potential client identified through marketing or direct outreach | CRM / Marketing |
| Lead Score | A numerical value indicating the likelihood of a lead converting to a client | Marketing |
| Legal Term | Clauses, warranties, liabilities, and legal conditions of a contract | Contract |
| Lesson Learned | A documented insight or takeaway from a completed project | Project |
| LPO | Local Purchase Order — a purchase order issued to a local vendor | Procurement |

## M–O

| Term | Definition | Context |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Maintenance Schedule | A calendar of recurring maintenance tasks for installed systems | Maintenance |
| Margin | The difference between project revenue and direct costs, expressed as a percentage | Quotation / Project |
| Material Request | An internal requisition for materials needed for a project | Procurement |
| Meeting Note | A record of discussion and decisions made during a client or project meeting | Project |
| Notification | An alert or message delivered to a user or system | Notification |
| Opportunity | A qualified lead that has entered the sales pipeline | CRM |
| Order Acknowledgement | A vendor's written confirmation accepting a purchase order | Procurement |
| Organizational Unit | A department or team within Triangle Black | Administration |

## P–R

| Term | Definition | Context |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Payment Milestone | A scheduled payment tied to completion of a defined project phase | Contract |
| Performance Bond | A guarantee that a contractor will fulfill their contractual obligations | Contract |
| Policy | A business rule or constraint that governs behavior | All |
| Procurement Request | A formal request to procure goods or services | Procurement |
| Proposal | A formal document outlining the solution, scope, and pricing for a client | Proposal |
| Purchase Order | A legally binding document authorizing a vendor to supply goods or services | Procurement |
| Quotation | A priced offer for a defined scope of work or goods | Quotation |
| RFQ | Request for Quotation — a document sent to vendors requesting pricing | Procurement |
| Renewal | The process of extending or renegotiating an existing contract | Renewals |

## S–Z

| Term | Definition | Context |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Scope Creep | Uncontrolled changes or additions to project scope without formal approval | Project |
| Service Report | A document detailing work performed during a maintenance visit | Maintenance |
| Site Survey | A physical inspection of a hotel to assess engineering and facility conditions | Site Survey |
| SLA | Service Level Agreement — performance commitments in a contract | Contract / Maintenance |
| Statement of Work | A detailed description of deliverables and activities for a project | Contract |
| Subcontractor | A third-party engaged to perform specific work under a contract | Project |
| Supplier | A vendor organization providing goods or services | Procurement |
| Technical Specification | A detailed document defining technical requirements and standards | Engineering Assessment |
| Tender | A formal invitation for suppliers to bid on a scope of work | Procurement |
| Timeline | A schedule of milestones and deadlines for a project | Project |
| User | An individual with access to the system, either internal or external | Administration |
| Value Object | An immutable domain object defined by its attributes rather than identity | All |
| Vendor | A supplier organization registered in the procurement system | Procurement |
| Warranty | A guarantee provided by Triangle Black or a vendor regarding workmanship or materials | Contract / Handover |
| Work Log | A daily record of labour and activities on a project | Project |
| Workflow | A sequence of steps or tasks that produce a defined outcome | All |
