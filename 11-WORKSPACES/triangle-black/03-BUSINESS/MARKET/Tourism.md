|------------|----------------------|---------------------|

## Implications for Triangle Black

1. **Peak season reliability is non-negotiable** — Hotels will pay premium for engineering partners who guarantee uptime during high-occupancy winter months
2. **Low-season opportunity** — May–September offers the ideal window for capex projects, renovations, and deep preventive maintenance
3. **Russian market dependency** creates risk — Triangle Black should monitor geopolitical developments affecting Russian travel to Egypt
4. **GCC and domestic tourism growth** provides year-round demand buffer, particularly for luxury and wellness segments
5. **Airport expansion** (SSH capacity of 10M) supports the room pipeline growth in Sharm El Sheikh
6. **MICE and business tourism** segments bring higher maintenance standards — convention hotels require rigorous MEP performance

## Risks

- Geopolitical instability in the Middle East affecting tourism demand
- Global recession reducing European outbound travel
- Currency volatility affecting inbound tour operator pricing
- Climate change impact on summer heat extremes
- Competition from Turkey, Greece, and Spain for European tourists
