## Sources

All market data sourced from industry reports by STR Global, Colliers International, CBRE, EHDR (Egyptian Hotel Development Report), Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities, WTTC, and published analyst research (2024–2026). Where specific figures are estimated, this is noted inline.

## Business Rules

- All market sizing uses USD unless noted
- CAGR calculations based on published analyst consensus ranges
- Hotel pipeline data sourced from STR Global (2025–2026)
- Tourism figures reference CAPMAS (Central Agency for Public Mobilization and Statistics) where possible
- Estimates are marked with (e) where official data is unavailable

## AI Opportunities

- Automated market monitoring via RSS feeds and news aggregation
- Competitor tracking with sentiment analysis on hotel engineering RFPs
- Dynamic pricing models for engineering services per hotel segment
- Occupancy-to-maintenance-demand correlation models

## Risks

- Market data is aggregated from multiple sources with varying methodologies
- Tourism recovery is subject to geopolitical stability and global economic conditions
- Hotel pipeline completion rates may vary based on financing and regulatory approvals
- Currency fluctuations (EGP/USD) affect market sizing in dollar terms
