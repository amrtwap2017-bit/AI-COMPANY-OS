# Hotel Procurement and Supply Chain Market

## Market Overview

Hotel procurement in Egypt encompasses all purchasing activities required to operate, maintain, and renovate hotel properties. This includes MEP equipment, spare parts, consumables, FF&E (Furniture, Fixtures & Equipment), OS&E (Operating Supplies & Equipment), and contracted services.

## Supply Chain Structure

### Channel Structure

```
Manufacturer / OEM (International)
    │
    ├── Authorized Distributor (Egypt)
    │        │
    │        ├── Hotel Direct Purchase
    │        ├── Agent / Reseller
    │        └── E-commerce Platform
    │
    ├── Parallel Importer (non-authorized)
    │        │
    │        └── Hotel Direct or Through Agent
    │
    └── Regional Distributor (Dubai/GCC)
             │
             └── Re-export to Egypt via Agent
```

### Key Characteristics

| Characteristic | Current State | Impact on Hotels |
|---------------|--------------|------------------|
| Fragmentation | High — 200+ suppliers serving hotel sector | Difficult to compare, high transaction costs |
| Digital maturity | Low — <5% of procurement is digital | Manual ordering, phone/fax, WhatsApp |
| Payment terms | Cash/advance for imported goods; 30–60 days for local | Working capital pressure |
| Lead times | 2–8 weeks for imported items (plus customs clearance) | Extended downtime for repairs |
| Minimum order quantities | Common for OEM parts | Overstocking or inability to order |
| After-sales support | Limited for imported brands | Hotels rely on in-house repair |
| Price transparency | Low — significant variation between suppliers | Inconsistent pricing, overpayment risk |
| Quality assurance | Inconsistent — counterfeit parts present | Equipment damage, shortened lifespan |

## Import Process & Challenges

### Customs Clearance Steps for MEP Equipment

| Step | Typical Duration | Cost Factor |
|------|-----------------|-------------|
| Supplier invoice & documentation | 1–3 days | Included in purchase price |
| Letter of Credit / Payment processing | 3–7 days | 1–3% bank fees |
| Shipping (Dubai to Egypt) | 3–5 days | USD 500–2,500 per container |
| Arrival at port (Alexandria / Sokhna / Damietta) | 1 day | — |
| Customs inspection & clearance | 5–15 days | 5–30% duties + VAT (14%) |
| Inland transport to Sharm El Sheikh | 2–5 days | USD 500–1,500 |
| **Total (typical)** | **15–36 days** | **20–45% on top of FOB cost** |

## Local vs International Sourcing

### Decision Factors

| Factor | Local Sourcing | International Sourcing |
|--------|---------------|----------------------|
| Lead time | 1–14 days | 15–36 days |
| Payment terms | 30–60 days net | LC / advance |
| Price (lower = better) | Variable (sometimes lower) | FOB cost often lower |
| Quality consistency | Inconsistent | Consistent (OEM) |
| Technical support | Limited | Often available |
| Warranty | Limited (30–90 days) | 12–24 months (OEM) |
| Customs / paperwork | None | Significant |
| Minimum order | Lower | Higher |
| Return policy | Limited | Better (for authorized distributors) |

### Optimal Sourcing Strategy by Item

| Item Type | Recommended Source | Rationale |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------|
| High-value / critical equipment | International (OEM authorized) | Quality, warranty, reliability |
| Commodity consumables | Local | Speed, lower logistics cost |
| Brand-specific parts | International (authorized) | Compatibility, warranty preservation |
| Emergency / breakdown parts | Local priority; international premium | Speed is critical |
| Bulk standard items (fittings, wire) | Local | Available, cheaper |
| Specialized / rare items | International | Not available locally |

## Implications for Triangle Black

1. **Procurement services** should be a primary margin driver — 20–30% margins significantly higher than labor-based engineering services (10–15%)
2. **Working capital is required** to pre-position inventory — initial inventory investment of USD 50K–100K for critical spare parts
3. **Supply chain relationships** with key distributors (Trane, Carrier, Siemens, Grundfos) are a priority for Year 1
4. **Digital procurement platform** differentiates Triangle Black from traditional procurement agents — invest in Year 1 MVP procurement module
5. **Emergency parts pool** creates immediate value and builds trust with hotels — high-visibility pain point
6. **Import expertise** (customs clearance, LC processing) is a translatable competitive advantage
7. **Volume aggregation** across multiple hotels is the key to supplier leverage — critical mass of 10+ hotels needed
