# Hotel Engineering Services Market

## Market Definition

The hotel engineering services market encompasses all activities related to the design, installation, operation, maintenance, and optimization of a hotel's mechanical, electrical, plumbing (MEP), and building systems. This includes preventive maintenance, reactive repairs, capital replacements, energy management, and system upgrades.

### Labor Market Implications

- **Skilled technician shortage** is acute for HVAC, electrical, and BMS roles — this creates a natural outsourcing incentive
- **Chief Engineers** are difficult to retain — average tenure in Sharm is 1.5–3 years
- **Training investment** is required — local technical institutes produce 50–100 graduates annually, but practical skills are limited
- **Seasonal variation** — some technicians leave Sharm during summer low season; year-round employment requires retention incentives

## Implications for Triangle Black

1. **Large, fragmented market** — USD 1B+ TAM with no dominant provider creates significant opportunity
2. **Skilled labor shortage** is the strongest tailwind for outsourcing — hotels struggle to find and retain qualified engineers
3. **Energy management** represents the highest-value add-on service — 15–35% savings potential creates compelling ROI for clients
4. **Capital project pipeline** (new build + renovation) provides lump-sum revenue alongside recurring operational contracts
5. **Preventive maintenance** is undervalued by most hotels — education and proven ROI can shift behavior
6. **Pricing model flexibility** will be critical — different segments prefer different models
