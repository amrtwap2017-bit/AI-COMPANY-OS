# Plumbing Systems

## Overview

Plumbing is the circulatory system of a hotel. It encompasses fresh water supply, wastewater drainage, hot water generation, gas systems, and irrigation. Plumbing failures — particularly leaks and blockages — cause significant property damage and guest disruption.

## Water Supply System

### Sourced Water
- **Municipal supply** (limited in Sharm El Sheikh)
- **Well water** (common in Sharm, high TDS — 2000-5000+ ppm)
- **Desalinated water** (RO plants — many Sharm resorts have their own)
- **Trucked water** (supplementary during peak season)

### Water Storage

| Tank Type | Typical Capacity | Location | Material |
|---|---|---|---|
| Underground / Ground tank | 100-1000 m³ | Basement | Concrete (lined), steel |
| Roof tank | 10-50 m³ | Roof | Stainless steel, GRP |
| Pressure tank | 1000-5000 L | Plant room | Steel (bladder type) |
| Booster set | Variable | Plant room | Multi-stage pumps + VFD |

### Pressure Boosting
- **Requirement:** 3-4 bar at highest fixture on top floor
- **Booster set:** Multiple pumps in parallel (Duty + Standby + Assist)
- **VFD control:** Maintains constant pressure regardless of demand
- **Pressure reducing valves (PRV):** Reduce pressure at lower floors to prevent water hammer and fixture damage

## Hot Water Generation

### Central Hot Water Systems

| System | Fuel | Best For | Efficiency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shell & Tube Heat Exchanger | Boiler (LPG/Diesel) | Large hotels | 80-85% |
| Plate Heat Exchanger | Boiler or District Heating | Mid-size hotels | 85-92% |
| Storage Calorifier | Electric or Steam | Small hotels | 95%+ (electric) |
| Solar Thermal | Solar (with backup) | Sunny climates | 50-70% solar fraction |
| Heat Pump | Electric | Energy-efficient hotels | COP 3-4 |

### Domestic Hot Water (DHW) Recirculation
- Continuous circulation loop to maintain instant hot water at all fixtures
- Recirculation pump runs 24/7 (often on timer during low occupancy)
- Return temperature: > 55°C (to prevent Legionella growth)
- Dead-leg from recirculation loop to fixture: Max 10 seconds draw

### Legionella Control
- Store water at > 60°C
- Return water at > 55°C
- Thermal shock (flush at > 70°C) monthly for low-use fixtures
- Chemical treatment (chlorine dioxide, copper-silver ionization)
- Regular testing (quarterly in most hotel chains)

## Drainage Systems

### Soil & Waste Drainage
- **Black water:** Toilets (gravity drainage, usually PVC or cast iron)
- **Gray water:** Sinks, showers, baths (separate where recycling is installed)
- **Vent piping:** Maintains atmospheric pressure, prevents trap siphonage
- **Floor drains:** In bathrooms, kitchens, plant rooms, pool areas
- **Grease traps:** Required before kitchen wastewater enters main drain

### Grease Management

| Component | Location | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Grease trap / interceptor | Kitchen floor | Fat, oil, grease separation |
| Bio-dosing system | Downstream of trap | Bacteria that consume FOG |
| Grease extractor hood | Above cooking | Captures airborne grease |
| Grease disposal contract | External | Periodic grease waste removal |

**Common issue:** Grease trap not cleaned → blockage in main drain → sewage backup → restaurant closure.

### Sewage Ejector Pumps
- Required where wastewater is below municipal sewer level
- Grinder pump (kitchen) or ejector pump (general)
- Duplex configuration (Duty + Standby)
- High-level alarm for pump failure

### Storm Water Drainage
- Roof drains, terrace drains, parking drains
- Storm water is typically separate from sewage
- Retention/detention tanks for heavy rainfall events (rare in Sharm)

## Plumbing Fixtures

### Guest Room Fixtures

| Fixture | Material | Typical Issues |
|---|---|---|
| Toilet (water closet) | Ceramic | Flush mechanism failure, leaking at base, blockage |
| Washbasin | Ceramic, stone | Faucet drip, drain clog, stopper failure |
| Shower | Tile, acrylic, glass | Tile grout failure, shower head calcification, drain slow |
| Bathtub | Acrylic, steel, stone | Drain mechanism, sealant failure |
| Bidets | Ceramic | Supply hose leak |
| Faucets | Brass, chrome | Cartridge wear, aerator clog |

**Water pressure:** Guest comfort threshold: 2-4 bar. Below 1.5 bar = complaints.

### Public Area Fixtures
- Restroom faucets: Sensor-operated (touchless) for hygiene
- Urinals: Waterless or flush valve
- Floor drains: Required in all wet areas
- Hose bibs: Every 30m on exterior, plant rooms, housekeeping rooms

### Kitchen Fixtures
- Pre-rinse sprayer (dishwash area)
- Pot fillers (above stoves)
- Mop sinks (housekeeping closets)
- Floor drains with trap primers

## Irrigation Systems

### Types

| Type | Typical Use | Efficiency |
|---|---|---|
| Drip irrigation | Landscaped areas | 90%+ |
| Pop-up sprinklers | Lawns, large areas | 70-80% |
| Micro-sprayers | Flower beds | 80% |
| Manual hosing | Small garden areas | 50% |

### Irrigation Control
- Timer-based controller (typical: 2-4 AM watering to minimize evaporation)
- Rain sensor (override during rain — rare in Sharm)
- Soil moisture sensors (more precise control)
- Weather-based controller (ET-based — evapotranspiration)

### Source
- Treated gray water (where system is installed) — preferred
- Well water (common in Sharm)
- Municipal water (expensive for irrigation)
- Desalinated reject water (brine, can be used for salt-tolerant plants)

## Gas Systems (LPG / Natural Gas)

### LPG (Common in Sharm — no natural gas grid)
- Bulk storage tank (500-5000 kg capacity, above-ground or underground)
- Vaporizer (converts liquid LPG to gas)
- Gas manifold with regulators
- Piping (steel or copper) to consumers (kitchen, boilers, laundry)
- Gas detectors at each consumer location
- Emergency shut-off valve (at tank + remote solenoid in kitchen)

### Safety Requirements
- Gas detection system with alarms (set at 20% LFL)
- Automatic shut-off on gas detection
- Explosion-proof equipment in gas areas
- Periodic leak testing
- Civil Defense inspection

## Common Plumbing Issues in Hotels

| Issue | Cause | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| Low water pressure | Scale buildup in pipes, failing booster pump | Guest complaints on every floor |
| Hot water inconsistency | Recirculation pump failure, calorifier scaling | Guest complaints in showers |
| Drain blockage | Hair, grease, soap, debris | Room out of service |
| Toilet overflow | Blockage, flush mechanism failure | Water damage, room out of service |
| Leaking pipe (hidden) | Corrosion, joint failure, ground movement | Ceiling damage, mold, structural risk |
| Water hammer | High velocity, air in pipes, faulty PRV | Pipe damage, noise complaints |
| Backflow | Cross-connection, pressure reversal | Contamination of potable water |
| Scale in pipes | Hard water (Sharm: 2000+ ppm TDS) | Reduced flow, equipment damage |
| Legionella risk | Stagnant water, low temperature | Health hazard, legal liability |
| Irrigation valve failure | Sand, debris, solenoid failure | Over/under-watering, landscape damage |

## Standards & Regulations

- **ASHRAE 191:** Legionella risk management for buildings
- **ASHRAE 188:** Legionellosis risk management
- **Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC)** or **International Plumbing Code (IPC)**
- **NSF/ANSI 61:** Drinking water system components
- **Egyptian Standard for Potable Water:** ES 180/2005
- **Egyptian Sanitary Code:** Wastewater discharge standards
- **NFPA 54:** National Fuel Gas Code
- **NFPA 58:** Liquefied Petroleum Gas Code

## Maintenance Schedule (Summary)

| Item | Frequency | Task |
|---|---|---|
| Faucet aerator cleaning | Monthly | Remove sediment buildup |
| Drain cleaning (showers) | Monthly | Hair and debris removal |
| Toilet flush mechanism | Quarterly | Adjust, replace flapper |
| Water heater / calorifier | Quarterly | Flush, check anode, T&P valve |
| Recirculation pump | Monthly | Check operation, log temperature |
| Grease trap | Weekly (kitchen) | Empty and clean |
| Backflow preventer test | Annual | Certified test |
| Storage tank cleaning | Annual | Drain, clean, disinfect |
| Hot water temperature check | Weekly | > 60°C at heater, > 55°C return |
| Legionella testing | Quarterly | Water sampling and lab test |
| Irrigation system | Monthly | Check heads, leaks, controller |

## AI Opportunities

- **Water Leak Detection:** Flow monitoring + ML to identify real-time leaks (significant change in minimum night flow)
- **Drain Blockage Prediction:** Analyze drain flow rates, usage patterns, and cleaning history to predict blockages
- **Scale Buildup Monitoring:** Track pressure drop across heat exchangers and pipes to predict descaling need
- **Water Pressure Optimization:** ML to predict pressure demands based on occupancy and time of day → optimize booster pump speed
- **Hot Water Demand Forecasting:** Predict DHW demand by hour to optimize heating schedule and reduce energy waste
- **Fixture Health Monitoring:** Track usage cycles of faucets, toilets, flush valves → predict failure and replace proactively
- **Leak Localization:** Acoustic sensors + ML to pinpoint leak location in distribution pipes
- **Irrigation Optimization:** Weather + soil moisture + evapotranspiration model to optimize watering schedule and reduce water consumption
