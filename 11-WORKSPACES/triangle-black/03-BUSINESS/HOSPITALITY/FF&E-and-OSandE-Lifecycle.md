## Definitions

| Term | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| FF&E | Furniture, Fixtures, and Equipment | Beds, sofas, tables, lamps, artwork, carpets, curtains |
| OS&E | Operating Supplies and Equipment | Linens, towels, glassware, china, silverware, uniforms |

---

## FF&E Lifecycle

### Phase 1: Selection & Procurement
- Brand standards (for branded hotels) or owner specifications
- Durability rating required for hospitality-grade (commercial, not residential)
- Lead time: 8-16 weeks typically
- Samples approved before bulk order

### Phase 2: Installation
- Coordinate with renovation schedule
- Protect during construction phase
- Install after painting and flooring complete
- Inventory and tag before placement

### Phase 3: Maintenance
- Regular inspection for wear and damage
- Upholstery cleaning schedule
- Wood refinishing schedule
- Carpet deep cleaning schedule

### Phase 4: Replacement
| Item | Typical Lifecycle | Replacement Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Guest room furniture | 5-8 years | Visible wear, brand refresh |
| Carpet (guest room) | 3-5 years | Stains, wear patterns |
| Carpet (public area) | 3-5 years | Heavy traffic wear |
| Mattresses | 5-7 years | Sagging, guest complaints |
| Curtains and drapes | 3-5 years | Fading, damage |
| Artwork | 5-10 years | Refresh cycle |
| Lighting fixtures | 5-10 years | Style change |

---

## OS&E Lifecycle

### Phase 1: Procurement
- Par levels established per hotel (typically 3-5 par for linens)
- Embroidery/branding coordination
- Bulk ordering for cost efficiency

### Phase 2: Inventory Management
| Category | Min Par | Max Par | Count Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bed linens | 3 | 5 | Monthly |
| Bath towels | 3 | 5 | Monthly |
| Bathrobes | 2 | 3 | Quarterly |
| Glassware | 3 | 4 | Monthly |
| China | 3 | 4 | Monthly |
| Silverware | 3 | 4 | Monthly |

### Phase 3: Replacement
| Item | Typical Lifecycle | Replacement Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Bed sheets | 1-2 years | Stains, thinning, pilling |
| Bath towels | 1-2 years | Loss of absorbency, fraying |
| Bathrobes | 2-3 years | Thinning, color fade |
| Glassware | 6-12 months | Chipping, scratching |
| China | 12-18 months | Chipping, cracking |
| Silverware | 12-18 months | Bent, scratched |

---

## Role of Engineering in FF&E/OS&E

- Engineering is responsible for installation quality of FF&E
- Engineering maintains OS&E storage areas (shelving, climate, security)
- Engineering disposes of replaced items per sustainability policy
- Engineering tracks warranty information for all FF&E items
