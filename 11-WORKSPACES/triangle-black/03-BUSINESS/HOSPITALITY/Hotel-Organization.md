# Hotel Organization

## Overview

A hotel is a 24/7/365 operation that combines hospitality services with physical infrastructure management. Understanding the organizational structure is critical because Triangle Black's services intersect with multiple departments — primarily Engineering, but also Procurement, Finance, Housekeeping, F&B, and Front Office.

## Typical Organizational Structure (Full-Service Hotel)

```
Hotel Owner / Asset Manager
        │
    General Manager (GM)
        │
┌───────┼───────────────────┬──────────────┬──────────────┐
│       │                   │              │              │
Dir. of  Dir. of     Executive    Dir. of    Dir. of
Rooms    F&B          Chef       Finance    Engineering
  │        │            │            │            │
  │        │            │            │            ├── Asst. Chief Eng.
  │        │            │            │            ├── Shift Engineers
  │        │            │            │            ├── Technicians
  │        │            │            │            └── Admin/Clerk
  │        │            │            │

Office    & Bars        Teams      Manager
│                                │
├─ Reservations                  ├─ Storekeeper
├─ Concierge                     ├─ Receiving Clerk
├─ Bell/Door                     └─ Buyer
├─ Guest Services

```

## Owner / Asset Manager

- Owns the physical asset (building, land, FF&E)
- Appoints the GM or management company
- Reviews financial performance, approves capital expenditures
- Concerned with asset value, ROI, brand compliance

## General Manager (GM)

- Highest operational authority
- Reports to Owner / Asset Manager or Management Company Regional VP
- Accountable for: Revenue, GOP (Gross Operating Profit), guest satisfaction, brand standards, asset condition
- Approves all capital expenditure requests above threshold
- Holds weekly executive committee (ExCom) meetings

## Executive Committee (ExCom)

Weekly meeting of all department heads. Typical attendees:
- GM (chairs)
- Director of Rooms (or Front Office Manager)
- Director of F&B
- Executive Chef
- Director of Finance / Financial Controller
- Director of Engineering (Chief Engineer)
- Director of Housekeeping
- Director of Sales & Marketing
- Director of HR
- Head of Security / Safety

## Department Breakdown

### Rooms Division
- **Front Office:** Check-in/out, reservations, concierge, guest services, bell desk
- **Housekeeping:** Room cleaning, public area cleaning, laundry, linen management
- **Guest Services:** Guest relations, VIP handling, special requests

### Food & Beverage
- **Kitchen:** All food production, cold/hot kitchens, pastry, butchery
- **Restaurants:** Outlets, bars, room service, banquet operations
- **Stewarding:** Dishwashing, pot wash, kitchen cleaning

### Engineering
- **Maintenance:** Preventive, corrective, emergency
- **MEP Systems:** HVAC, electrical, plumbing, fire fighting
- **Projects:** Renovations, upgrades, capital works
- **Energy:** Monitoring, conservation, utility management
- **Grounds:** Landscaping, pool maintenance, external areas
- **Workshop:** Fabrication, repair, carpentry

### Finance
- **Accounting:** AP, AR, general ledger
- **Purchasing:** Procurement, receiving, storekeeping
- **Cost Control:** F&B cost, inventory, waste
- **IT:** Network, systems, property management system (PMS)

## Decision-Making Hierarchy by Spend Level

| Spend Level | Approval Authority | Typical Items |
|---|---|---|
| < $1,000 | Department Head | Minor repairs, consumables, tools |
| $1,000 - $5,000 | Financial Controller + Dept Head | Standard maintenance parts, small services |
| $5,000 - $15,000 | General Manager | Equipment replacement, contractor services |
| $15,000 - $50,000 | GM + Owner Rep | Major repairs, system overhauls |
| > $50,000 | Owner / Asset Manager | Capital projects, renovations |

## Key Stakeholders for Triangle Black

### The Chief Engineer (CE)
- Primary day-to-day stakeholder
- Knows every system, every problem, and every pain point
- Often under-resourced and over-stretched
- Valuable source of truth for procurement needs and asset condition

### The Financial Controller (FC)
- Controls the budget and purchasing approvals
- Concerned with cost savings, predictability, and compliance
- Must be convinced of ROI and cost reduction

### The General Manager (GM)
- Ultimate decision-maker for partnership agreements
- Cares about guest satisfaction scores, cost reduction, and reducing management headaches
- Speaks the language of GOP and EBITDA

### The Purchasing Manager
- Processes purchase orders and manages supplier relationships
- May see Triangle Black as a competitor or a complement
- Important to align incentives

## Hotel Types and Structure Variations

### Limited Service / Select Service (e.g., Holiday Inn Express)
- No F&B department (or minimal)
- Smaller engineering team (1-3 people)
- GM may also manage engineering oversight
- Flatter structure, faster decisions

### Luxury / Full Service (e.g., Four Seasons, Ritz-Carlton)
- Large engineering team (15-40+ people)
- Dedicated HVAC, electrical, plumbing specialists
- Formal approval chains, longer decision cycles
- Higher brand compliance requirements

### Resort (e.g., Sharm El Sheikh beach resorts)
- Additional departments: Recreation, Animation, Water Sports
- Larger grounds maintenance, multiple pools, extensive landscaping
- Higher energy consumption (AC running almost year-round)
- More complex water systems (desalination, treatment)

### Chain vs. Independent
- **Chain:** Standardized operating procedures, brand-mandated suppliers, regional procurement
- **Independent:** More flexibility, less bureaucracy, but less purchasing power
