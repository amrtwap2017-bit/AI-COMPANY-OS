## Definitions

| Term | Definition | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| CAPEX | Capital Expenditure — investment in assets with useful life > 1 year | New chiller, HVAC replacement, roof renovation |
| OPEX | Operational Expenditure — ongoing costs to operate and maintain | Repair parts, labor, chemicals, filters, refrigerants |

---

## Decision Framework

| Question | If Yes → | If No → |
|---|---|---|
| Does this extend asset life by 3+ years? | CAPEX | OPEX |
| Does this improve efficiency by > 15%? | CAPEX | Evaluate further |
| Is this a replacement of failed component? | OPEX (repair) | — |
| Is this a like-for-like replacement? | OPEX | CAPEX (upgrade) |
| Is this required by regulation/code? | CAPEX | — |
| Useful life > 1 year and cost > threshold? | CAPEX | OPEX |

## Hotel Engineering Budget Allocation

| Category | Typical % of Engineering Budget | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Salaries and labor | 40-50% | OPEX |
| Maintenance materials and parts | 15-20% | OPEX |
| Utilities and energy | 10-15% | OPEX |
| External contractors | 5-10% | OPEX/CAPEX |
| Equipment replacement | 5-10% | CAPEX |
| Renovation and upgrades | 5-10% | CAPEX |
| Safety and compliance | 2-5% | CAPEX/OPEX |
| Training and tools | 1-2% | OPEX |

---

## CAPEX Approval Process

1. Engineering identifies need (condition assessment, failure history, regulatory requirement)
2. Business case prepared: cost, benefit, ROI, payback period
3. Financial controller reviews budget availability
4. GM/owner approves per authorization matrix
5. Procurement executed per Triangle Black procurement process
