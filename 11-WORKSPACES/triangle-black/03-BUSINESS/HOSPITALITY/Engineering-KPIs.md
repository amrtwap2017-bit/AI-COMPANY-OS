# Engineering KPIs

## Overview

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for hotel engineering transform operational data into actionable management information. They enable the Chief Engineer to track performance, identify problems, justify budget requests, and communicate value to the GM and ownership. KPIs are organized into categories covering maintenance, energy, procurement, asset management, team performance, and guest satisfaction.

## KPI Framework

```
Strategic (GM / Owner level) → Annually / Quarterly
    ↓
Tactical (Chief Engineer) → Monthly
    ↓
Operational (Technicians / Shifts) → Daily / Weekly
```

## Maintenance KPIs

### PM Compliance & Execution

| KPI | Formula | Target | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| PM Completion Rate | PMs completed on time / Total PMs due | > 90% | Core indicator of maintenance discipline |
| PM Backlog | Overdue PMs (aging) | < 5% of total PMs | Growing backlog = deferred maintenance |
| PM Schedule Compliance | PMs completed in scheduled week / Total PMs | > 85% | Schedule adherence |
| Emergency Work % | Emergency work orders / Total work orders | < 10% | > 10% indicates reactive culture |
| Planned vs. Unplanned Ratio | Planned hours / Unplanned hours | > 70:30 | Measure of proactivity |

### Work Order Performance

| KPI | Formula | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Work Order Backlog | Open work orders / Available technicians | < 10 per tech |
| Work Order Aging | Average days since work order opened | < 7 days |
| First-Time Fix Rate | WOs fixed on first visit / Total WOs | > 80% |
| MTTR (Mean Time To Repair) | Total repair time / Number of repairs | < 4 hours (P1), < 24 hours (P2) |
| MTBF (Mean Time Between Failures) | Total operating time / Number of failures | > 1000 hours (critical systems) |
| Availability | MTBF / (MTBF + MTTR) | > 98% |
| Rework Rate | WOs requiring follow-up / Total WOs | < 5% |

### Guest Room Maintenance

| KPI | Formula | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Room Defects per Inspection | Number of defects found / Rooms inspected | < 2 per room |
| Turnaround Time (Guest Complaint) | Time from complaint to resolution | < 30 min (P1), < 2 hours (P2) |
| Repeat Guest Complaints | Rooms with > 1 complaint in 30 days | < 1% |
| Rooms Out of Service (OOO) | OOO rooms / Total rooms (engineering-related) | < 1% |
| Preventive Maintenance per Room | PM visits done / Total rooms | 4-6 per year per room |

## Energy KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Energy Use Intensity (EUI) | Total kWh / m² GFA / year | < 350 (hot climate) |
| Energy per Occupied Room | Total kWh / Occupied room nights | < 80 kWh |
| Energy Cost % of Revenue | Total energy cost / Hotel revenue | < 6% |
| Peak Demand | Max kW in any 15-min period | Reduce year-over-year |
| Power Factor | kW / kVA | > 0.95 |
| Water Consumption per Occupied Room | m³ / Occupied room night | < 0.8 m³ |
| Carbon Intensity | kg CO₂ / Occupied room night | < 30 kg CO₂ |

### Energy Trend Indicators

| KPI | Comparison | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Year-over-Year Consumption | Current month vs. same month last year | Monthly |
| Degree-Day Normalized | Consumption adjusted for weather variation | Monthly |
| Cost Avoidance | Savings from ECMs implemented | Monthly |
| Benchmark Score | Compare to similar hotels (climate, size) | Annually |

## Procurement KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Emergency Purchase % | Emergency POs / Total POs | < 10% |
| Procurement Cost Savings | (Market price - Price paid) / Market price | > 10% |
| PO Cycle Time | Time from PR approval to PO issue | < 24 hours (standard), < 4 hours (emergency) |
| Supplier On-Time Delivery | Deliveries on time / Total deliveries | > 95% |
| Supplier Quality | Rejected deliveries / Total deliveries | < 2% |
| Days Payable Outstanding | (Trade creditors / Annual purchases) × 365 | 60-90 days |
| Inventory Turnover | COGS / Average inventory value | 6-12 turns per year |
| Stockout Rate | Items out of stock / Total stocked items | < 2% |

### Inventory KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Stock Accuracy | Physical count matches system count | > 98% |
| Slow-Moving Stock % | Items not moved in 12 months / Total items | < 10% |
| Reorder Point Compliance | Orders placed at or before reorder point | > 95% |
| Value of Aged Inventory | Inventory > 6 months old | < 5% of total inventory value |

## Asset Management KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Asset Register Completeness | Assets in system / Physical assets | > 95% |
| Asset Condition Index | Average condition rating (1-5, 1=best) | < 2.5 |
| Maintenance Cost % of Asset Value | Annual maintenance cost / Asset replacement value | < 5% |
| Critical Asset Availability | Uptime / Total time (critical assets only) | > 99% |
| Asset Lifecycle Adherence | Assets replaced within expected life ± 20% | > 80% |
| Warranty Recovery Rate | Warranty claims approved / Warranty claims filed | > 90% |

## Financial KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering Cost per Occupied Room | Total engineering cost / Occupied room nights | $5-15 (varies by hotel type) |
| Engineering Cost % of Total Revenue | Engineering opex / Hotel revenue | 4-8% |
| Maintenance Cost per m² | Total maintenance cost / m² GFA | $10-25 |
| Energy Cost per m² | Total energy cost / m² GFA | $8-20 |
| GOP (Gross Operating Profit) Impact | GOP with TB vs. baseline | +2-5% |
| ROI on Energy Projects | (Annual savings - Annual cost) / Investment | > 15% |

## Team Performance KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Productivity (Work Orders per Tech per Day) | Completed WOs / Technician days worked | 3-5 |
| Overtime % | Overtime hours / Total hours | < 10% |
| Training Hours per Technician | Total training hours / Number of technicians | > 40 hours/year |
| First-Call Resolution Rate | Issues resolved on first visit | > 80% |
| Response Time (P1) | Time from notification to arrival on site | < 10 minutes |
| Technician Utilization | Billable hours / Available hours | 75-85% |

## Guest Satisfaction KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering-Related Guest Complaints | Complaints about room systems / Total occupied rooms | < 1% |
| AC / Temperature Complaints | Temperature complaints / Total occupied rooms | < 0.5% |
| Water Pressure Complaints | Water pressure complaints / Total occupied rooms | < 0.3% |
| Noise Complaints (Engineering-related) | Noise complaints / Total occupied rooms | < 0.2% |
| Maintenance Response Score | Guest survey question: "How quickly were issues resolved?" | > 4.5/5 |
| Zero-Defect Room Percentage | Rooms with zero maintenance issues during stay | > 90% |

## Compliance KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Fire Drill Completion | Drills completed / Required drills (annual) | 100% |
| Civil Defense Inspection Score | Score on inspection (if scored) | Pass (no critical) |
| Regulatory Certification Currency | Expired certificates / Total required certificates | 0% |
| LOTO Compliance | LOTO violations / Total maintenance tasks | 0 |
| Fire Alarm Test Compliance | Tests completed / Tests required (monthly/quarterly) | 100% |
| Chemical Safety Audit Score | Score from safety audit | > 90% |

## Dashboard Design

### Executive Dashboard (for GM)

Shows high-level KPIs on one page:
- Engineering cost to budget (YTD)
- Energy cost to budget (YTD)
- Emergency work order %
- Guest complaint rate (engineering-related)
- Critical asset availability
- PM completion rate
- Top 3 issues this month

### Chief Engineer Dashboard

More detailed, used for monthly ExCom report:
- All maintenance KPIs above
- Work order aging distribution
- Top 10 failure-prone assets
- Inventory stock-out alerts
- Team productivity and overtime
- Energy consumption vs. baseline
- Project status (on-time, on-budget)

### Technician Dashboard (Daily)

- Assigned work orders for today
- Priority order (P1 first)
- Parts needed (check availability)
- Equipment history relevant to assigned tasks
- Location maps / building floor

## AI Opportunities

- **Automated KPI Calculation:** Auto-compute all KPIs from raw data sources (CMMS, BMS, PMS, procurement system)
- **Anomaly Detection in KPIs:** ML detects unusual KPI movements (e.g., PM completion rate suddenly drops) and alerts management
- **Predictive KPI Forecasting:** Forecast future KPI values based on trends, seasonality, and planned actions
- **Root Cause Analysis on KPI Deviation:** When a KPI goes red, AI analyzes underlying factors and suggests corrective actions
- **Natural Language Report Generation:** Auto-generate monthly engineering report in natural language from KPI data
- **Benchmarking Intelligence:** Compare hotel KPIs against anonymized peer group and identify improvement areas
- **Early Warning System:** ML predicts which KPIs are likely to miss target next month → proactive management
- **Cascading KPI Trees:** Drill-down from high-level KPI (e.g., maintenance cost too high) to root cause (e.g., specific chiller with high MTTR)
