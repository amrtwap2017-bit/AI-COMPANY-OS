## Energy Audit Methodology

### Level 1 — Walk-Through Audit
| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| Effort | 1-2 days |
| Data | Utility bills analysis, visual inspection |
| Output | Preliminary savings estimate, low-cost opportunities |
| Accuracy | ±30% |
| Cost | Minimal |

### Level 2 — Detailed Audit
| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| Effort | 1-2 weeks |
| Data | Detailed energy consumption data, equipment inventory, operating schedules |
| Output | Detailed savings calculation, investment-grade recommendations |
| Accuracy | ±15% |
| Cost | Moderate |

### Level 3 — Investment-Grade Audit
| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| Effort | 2-4 weeks |
| Data | Sub-metering, data logging, detailed modeling |
| Output | Full business case with ROI, implementation plan, M&V plan |
| Accuracy | ±5% |
| Cost | Significant |

## Energy Audit Process

1. **Data Collection**
   - 12-24 months of utility bills
   - Sub-meter readings by zone/function
   - Equipment inventory and specifications
   - Operating schedules and occupancy data

2. **Benchmarking**
   - kWh/m²/year vs industry benchmarks
   - EUI (Energy Use Intensity) comparison
   - Weather-normalized comparison

3. **Site Inspection**
   - HVAC systems and controls
   - Lighting systems
   - Building envelope
   - Kitchen and laundry equipment
   - Water systems
   - Pool and spa

4. **Analysis**
   - End-use breakdown (HVAC, lighting, water heating, kitchen, laundry, other)
   - Energy conservation measures (ECMs) identified
   - Cost-benefit analysis per ECM
   - Simple payback, IRR, NPV

5. **Reporting**
   - Current energy performance
   - ECM recommendations with business case
   - Implementation roadmap
   - Monitoring and verification plan

## Common Energy Conservation Measures

| ECM | Typical Savings | Payback | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|
| LED lighting retrofit | 15-25% of lighting cost | 1-3 years | Easy |
| Guest room energy management (GRMS) | 20-35% of room energy | 2-4 years | Moderate |
| VFD on pumps and fans | 20-40% of motor energy | 1-3 years | Moderate |
| Chiller optimization | 10-20% of cooling energy | 2-4 years | Moderate |
| Boiler efficiency improvement | 5-15% of heating energy | 1-3 years | Easy |
| Solar water heating | 30-60% of water heating | 3-6 years | Significant |
| BMS optimization | 10-20% total energy | 1-3 years | Moderate |
| Pool cover (heated pools) | 50-70% of pool heating | 1-2 years | Easy |
| Kitchen hood optimization | 20-40% of kitchen ventilation | 2-4 years | Moderate |
| Laundry heat recovery | 20-30% of laundry energy | 2-4 years | Moderate |

## Sustainability Initiatives

### Quick Wins (0-6 months)
- LED lighting retrofit (all areas)
- Occupancy-based room temperature setbacks
- Kitchen hood scheduling (match to cooking hours)
- Pool temperature reduction (1°C = 10-15% heating savings)
- Laundry full-load optimization
- Guest towel and linen reuse program

### Medium Term (6-18 months)
- Solar water heating installation
- BMS upgrade and optimization
- VFD installation on major pumps and fans
- Guest room energy management system
- Water consumption monitoring and leak detection
- Waste segregation and recycling program

### Long Term (18-36 months)
- Solar PV installation (reduce grid dependence)
- Chiller plant optimization or replacement
- Building envelope improvements (insulation, glazing)
- Waste-to-energy for organic waste
- Electric vehicle charging stations
- Carbon offset program
- ISO 14001 (Environmental Management System) certification
