## Project Lifecycle in Hospitality

```
Design Phase (3-6 months)
  ├─ Concept Design — MEP load calculations, system selection
  ├─ Detailed Design — Drawings, specifications, BOQ
  └─ Tender — Contractor bidding, technical submittals

Construction Phase (12-24 months)
  ├─ Rough-In — MEP rough works, concealed installations
  ├─ Finishing — Fit-out, ceiling works, fixtures
  └─ Commissioning — System testing, balancing, BMS integration

Handover Phase (1-3 months)
  ├─ Snagging — Defect identification and rectification
  ├─ Documentation — O&M manuals, as-built drawings
  └─ Training — Owner facility team training
```

## Key Success Factors

1. **Material availability**: Egypt market has specific material lead times (import-dependency)
2. **Labor skill availability**: Specialized MEP labor market dynamics
3. **Hotel operation constraints**: Work must minimize guest disruption in operating hotels
4. **Consultant relationships**: Engineering consultants drive specification decisions
5. **Payment terms**: Hotel projects typically have milestone-based payment with retention
