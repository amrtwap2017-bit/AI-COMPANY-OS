# Engineering SOP — Standard Operating Procedure

## Purpose
Standardize the engineering work order lifecycle to ensure consistent task execution, quality assurance, and full traceability across all Triangle Black operations.

## Scope
All engineering and maintenance work performed by Triangle Black teams.

## Actors
- Requester — submits the work request (hotel staff, system, or client portal)
- Engineering Coordinator — triages, assigns, and monitors work orders
- Engineer / Technician — executes the work
- Quality Supervisor — inspects completed work
- Director of Engineering — oversees SLA compliance and escalations

## Process Flow

### 1. Work Request Intake
| Source | Method |
|--------|--------|
| Hotel staff | Client portal or mobile app |
| Preventive maintenance | Auto-generated by platform |
| Inspection finding | Created from inspection checklist |
| Emergency | Phone call + platform creation within 15 minutes |

### 2. Work Order Creation
| Step | Action | Owner |
|------|--------|-------|
| 2.1 | Request received and logged in system | Engineering Coordinator |
| 2.2 | Priority assigned: Emergency / High / Medium / Low | Engineering Coordinator |
| 2.3 | Category and sub-category selected from standard taxonomy | Engineering Coordinator |
| 2.4 | Asset linked (if applicable) | Engineering Coordinator |
| 2.5 | SLA timer starts based on priority | Platform |

### 3. Task Assignment
| Step | Action | Owner |
|------|--------|-------|
| 3.1 | Work order appears in unassigned queue | Engineering Coordinator |
| 3.2 | Assign to engineer based on skill, availability, location | Engineering Coordinator |
| 3.3 | Engineer receives push notification with details | Platform |
| 3.4 | Engineer accepts assignment | Engineer |
| 3.5 | If not accepted within 15 minutes, auto-assign or escalate | Platform |

### 4. Execution
| Step | Action | Owner |
|------|--------|-------|
| 4.1 | Engineer reviews work order details and checklist | Engineer |
| 4.2 | Engineer navigates to asset location (QR scan or map) | Engineer |
| 4.3 | Work performed per standard procedure | Engineer |
| 4.4 | Checklist items completed and signed off in app | Engineer |
| 4.5 | Photos of before/during/after captured in app | Engineer |
| 4.6 | Parts and materials consumed logged | Engineer |
| 4.7 | Work marked as complete with engineer signature | Engineer |

### 5. Quality Check
| Step | Action | Owner |
|------|--------|-------|
| 5.1 | Completed work order moves to quality queue | Platform |
| 5.2 | Quality Supervisor reviews photos, checklist, materials | Quality Supervisor |
| 5.3 | Physical inspection for critical or high-priority tasks | Quality Supervisor |
| 5.4 | Pass → work order approved and closed | Quality Supervisor |
| 5.5 | Fail → work order returned to engineer with comments | Quality Supervisor |
| 5.6 | Re-work completed and re-submitted for quality check | Engineer |

### 6. Completion and Closure
| Step | Action | Owner |
|------|--------|-------|
| 6.1 | All checklists complete and documents attached | Platform |
| 6.2 | Customer satisfaction survey sent (optional) | Platform |
| 6.3 | Work order status set to Closed | Platform |
| 6.4 | Work order archived with full audit trail | Platform |
| 6.5 | Asset maintenance history updated | Platform |

## SLA Tiers
| Priority | Response Time | Completion Time |
|----------|---------------|-----------------|
| Emergency | < 15 minutes | < 4 hours |
| High | < 30 minutes | < 24 hours |
| Medium | < 2 hours | < 72 hours |
| Low | < 8 hours | < 7 days |

## Business Rules
- Emergency work orders override all queue prioritization
- No work order closed without completed checklist
- Parts consumption must be logged within 1 hour of completion
- Quality check mandatory for High and Emergency work orders
- Engineer cannot close own work orders (segregation of duty)

## Inputs / Outputs
| Inputs | Outputs |
|--------|---------|
| Work request | Completed work order |
| Asset information | Quality inspection report |
| Checklist templates | Materials consumption log |
| Engineer availability | Asset maintenance history update |

## KPIs
| KPI | Target | Frequency |
|-----|--------|-----------|
| SLA compliance rate | > 95% | Weekly |
| First-time fix rate | > 85% | Monthly |
| Average response time | Within SLA | Weekly |
| Quality check pass rate | > 90% | Monthly |
| Work orders closed without rework | > 85% | Monthly |

## Exceptions
- Emergency bypass: Work can start before WO is created; WO must be created within 15 minutes
- Offline execution: Engineer can work offline; data syncs when connectivity returns
- Parts unavailable: Mark WO as On Hold — Parts; notify Procurement Officer
