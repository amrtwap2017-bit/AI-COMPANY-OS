# Sales SOP — Standard Operating Procedure

## Purpose
Define the end-to-end sales process for Triangle Black, covering lead generation through contract signing, ensuring consistent handling, CRM hygiene, and measurable pipeline management.

## Scope
All sales team members, sales support, and sales operations.

## Actors
- Sales Representative — lead qualification and engagement
- Sales Manager — pipeline review and approval authority
- Operations Director — proposal content and pricing sign-off
- CRM System — automated tracking, reminders, and reporting

## Process Flow

### 1. Lead Capture
| Step | Action | Owner | System |
|------|--------|-------|--------|
| 1.1 | Lead enters via website form, referral, event, or inbound call | All | CRM |
| 1.2 | Sales Rep validates lead data within 2 business hours | Sales Rep | CRM |
| 1.3 | Duplicate check — merge or flag in CRM | CRM | CRM |
| 1.4 | Lead assigned to Sales Rep by round-robin or territory | Sales Manager | CRM |

### 2. Lead Qualification
| Step | Action | Owner | Criteria |
|------|--------|-------|----------|
| 2.1 | Initial call within 4 business hours of assignment | Sales Rep | — |
| 2.2 | Confirm: hotel property, decision-maker, needs, budget, timeline | Sales Rep | BANT framework |
| 2.3 | Score lead: Hot / Warm / Cold | Sales Rep | CRM scoring |
| 2.4 | Cold leads moved to nurture sequence | CRM | Automated |

### 3. CRM Update
| Step | Action | Owner |
|------|--------|-------|
| 3.1 | Every interaction logged as activity in CRM | Sales Rep |
| 3.2 | Lead status updated: New → Contacted → Qualified → Opportunity | Sales Rep |
| 3.3 | Opportunity created with value, close date, probability | Sales Rep |
| 3.4 | Notes must include: next steps, date, stakeholder sentiment | Sales Rep |

### 4. Follow-Up Cadence
| Stage | Cadence | Method |
|-------|---------|--------|
| Lead — Not Contacted | Attempt 3 times in 5 days | Call + Email + Call |
| Lead — Contacted | Every 5 business days | Email / Call alternating |
| Opportunity — Active | Every 3 business days | Call + Email + Meeting |
| Proposal Sent | Follow up day 3, day 7, day 14 | Call |
| Negotiation | Daily or as agreed | Call / In-person |
| Closed Won | Handover to ops within 1 business day | Meeting |
| Closed Lost | Log reason, move to nurture within 7 days | CRM |

### 5. Proposal Generation
| Step | Action | Owner |
|------|--------|-------|
| 5.1 | Sales Rep drafts proposal using approved template | Sales Rep |
| 5.2 | Operations Director reviews scope and pricing | Ops Director |
| 5.3 | Sales Manager approves commercial terms | Sales Manager |
| 5.4 | Proposal delivered to client in-person or via portal | Sales Rep |
| 5.5 | Proposal logged in CRM with version tracking | Sales Rep |

### 6. Closing
| Step | Action | Owner |
|------|--------|-------|
| 6.1 | Negotiate terms within approved price band | Sales Rep |
| 6.2 | Legal review for contracts over threshold | Legal |
| 6.3 | Contract signed (electronic or wet) | Client + Sales Rep |
| 6.4 | Contract filed in CRM and document vault | Sales Operations |
| 6.5 | Kickoff meeting scheduled with delivery team | Sales Rep + Ops |

## Business Rules
- Minimum margin: 25% on all deals
- Discounts above 10% require Sales Manager approval
- Discounts above 20% require Operations Director approval
- All deals must have a signed contract before work begins
- No verbal commitments — all terms documented in CRM

## Inputs / Outputs
| Inputs | Outputs |
|--------|---------|
| Lead contact information | Qualified opportunity |
| Client requirements | Signed contract |
| BANT qualification data | CRM record with full history |
| Proposal documents | Handover document to operations |

## KPIs
| KPI | Target | Frequency |
|-----|--------|-----------|
| Lead response time | < 2 hours | Weekly |
| Lead-to-opportunity conversion | > 30% | Monthly |
| Proposal win rate | > 40% | Monthly |
| Average deal cycle | < 45 days | Monthly |
| CRM data completeness | 100% | Weekly |
| Follow-up compliance | > 95% | Weekly |

## Exceptions
- VIP leads (GM or above at major chain): Assign to Sales Manager directly
- Emergency service requests: Route to Operations, not Sales
- Multi-property group deals: Assign dedicated account team
