# Strategic Objective Management

## Overview

Strategic objectives are the cornerstone of enterprise portfolio management. They translate high-level vision and mission into measurable, time-bound outcomes that guide investment decisions and program execution. The Enterprise AI Execution System uses the Objectives and Key Results (OKR) framework to define, cascade, and track objectives across the organization.

## OKR Framework

The OKR framework is the standard methodology for setting and managing strategic objectives:

- **Objective:** A qualitative, inspirational goal that describes what the enterprise seeks to achieve. Objectives are ambitious, time-bound, and actionable.
- **Key Results:** Quantitative, measurable outcomes that indicate progress toward the objective. Key results are specific, verifiable, and time-bound.

Each objective has 3-5 key results. Key results are scored on a 0-1.0 scale (or 0-100%) at the end of each period, with 0.7-0.8 representing meaningful progress for stretch goals.

### OKR Principles

1. **Alignment:** Objectives cascade from enterprise to portfolio to program level, ensuring every team's work connects to strategic priorities.
2. **Transparency:** All objectives and key results are visible across the organization, reducing duplication and enabling collaboration.
3. **Ambition:** Stretch goals drive innovation and breakthrough thinking. Achieving 70% of a stretch goal often exceeds achieving 100% of a safe goal.
4. **Accountability:** Each objective has a named owner who is responsible for driving progress and reporting outcomes.
5. **Agility:** OKRs are set quarterly with annual themes, allowing adaptation as market conditions change.

## Objective Cascade

Objectives cascade through three organizational levels:

### Enterprise Objectives (Annual)
- Set by executive leadership
- Aligned to strategic pillars (3-5 year vision)
- 3-5 objectives covering the enterprise's top priorities
- Reviewed and refreshed annually

### Portfolio Objectives (Quarterly/Annual)
- Derived from enterprise objectives
- Specific to portfolio scope and strategic domain
- 3-5 objectives per portfolio
- Reviewed quarterly

### Program Objectives (Quarterly)
- Derived from portfolio objectives
- Specific to program deliverables and outcomes
- 2-4 objectives per program
- Reviewed monthly with quarterly formal assessment

## Key Result Measurement

Key results are measured using consistent scoring criteria:

| Score | Interpretation | Description |
|-------|----------------|-------------|
| 0.0 - 0.3 | Off Track | Minimal progress, significant intervention needed |
| 0.4 - 0.6 | Progressing | Partial achievement, on path but needs attention |
| 0.7 - 0.8 | On Track | Strong progress, likely to achieve target |
| 0.9 - 1.0 | Achieved | Target met or exceeded |

For stretch objectives, 0.7 is considered a good outcome. If all key results are consistently scored at 1.0, the objectives may not be ambitious enough.

## Objective Review Cadence

| Review Type | Frequency | Participants | Focus |
|-------------|-----------|--------------|-------|
| Weekly Check-in | Weekly | Objective owner, program managers | Progress updates, blocker identification |
| Monthly Review | Monthly | Portfolio review board | KR scoring, risk assessment, rebalancing |
| Quarterly Review | Quarterly | Executive leadership | Objective completion scoring, next quarter planning |
| Annual Review | Annually | Board, executive team | Strategy refresh, objective setting for next year |

## Program Alignment

Each program within the portfolio must be explicitly aligned to one or more strategic objectives. This alignment is documented in the program charter and tracked in the portfolio management system. Misaligned programs are candidates for deprioritization or termination.

The alignment between objectives and programs is bidirectional:
- **Top-down:** Objectives define what programs must achieve
- **Bottom-up:** Program results inform whether objectives are being met

This closed-loop approach ensures that strategy drives execution and execution validates strategy.
