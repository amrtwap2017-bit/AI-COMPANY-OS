# Definition of Ready

The Definition of Ready (DoR) defines the pre-conditions that must be satisfied before a sprint item (user story, task, spike, or bug) can be accepted into a sprint. All items must meet every applicable criterion before sprint planning begins.

## Requirements & Specification

- [ ] User story follows the INVEST principle (Independent, Negotiable, Valuable, Estimable, Small, Testable)
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are written in a clear, unambiguous format (Given/When/Then or equivalent)
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are testable with defined pass/fail conditions
- [ ] Non-functional requirements are explicitly documented (performance, security, scalability)
- [ ] All dependencies on other stories, teams, or external systems are identified
- [ ] Wireframes, mockups, or design prototypes are approved by stakeholders
- [ ] Edge cases and error scenarios are documented
- [ ] Regulatory or compliance requirements are identified and attached
- [ ] Data privacy implications are assessed and documented
- [ ] The story has been estimated in story points by the team

## Technical Readiness

- [ ] Architecture approach has been discussed and agreed upon
- [ ] Required infrastructure or environment changes are identified
- [ ] Database schema changes are reviewed and migration strategy exists
- [ ] Integration points with external services are documented with contract details
- [ ] Third-party APIs or libraries required are identified and approved
- [ ] Feature flags or toggles are identified if needed for dark launching
- [ ] Logging and monitoring requirements are specified
- [ ] Required test environments are available and configured

## Dependencies & External

- [ ] All external dependencies are resolved or have a target resolution date
- [ ] Dependent stories are also committed to the same sprint or explicitly deferred
- [ ] Access to external systems, credentials, or sandboxes has been requested
- [ ] Required vendor or partner coordination is in progress
- [ ] No blockers from other teams without an escalation path

## Team Capacity

- [ ] Team has sufficient capacity to complete the item within the sprint
- [ ] Required subject matter experts are available for consultation
- [ ] Required reviewers (security, architecture, QA) have been identified
- [ ] No critical skill gaps exist for the work required

## Definition Checklist

- [ ] Story is in the correct backlog refinement state per the team's workflow
- [ ] Story has a clear title and description
- [ ] Story is linked to its epic or feature
- [ ] Priority is assigned and aligned with the roadmap
- [ ] Story size is appropriate (can be completed within a single sprint)

## Approval

- [ ] Product Owner has reviewed and accepted the story
- [ ] Tech Lead or Architect has reviewed technical feasibility
- [ ] QA lead has confirmed testability
- [ ] Scrum Master has confirmed the story meets DoR criteria
