# 03-PROCUREMENT — KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Target |
|-----|---------|--------|
| PO cycle time | Avg days from requisition to PO approval | < 5 days |
| Supplier on-time delivery | Delivered on time / Total deliveries | ≥ 90% |
| Goods rejection rate | Rejected items / Total received | < 3% |
| Requisition-to-order conversion | POs created / Requisitions approved | ≥ 95% |
| Procurement savings | (Budget - Actual) / Budget × 100 | ≥ 5% |
| Emergency procurement ratio | Urgent POs / Total POs | < 15% |
