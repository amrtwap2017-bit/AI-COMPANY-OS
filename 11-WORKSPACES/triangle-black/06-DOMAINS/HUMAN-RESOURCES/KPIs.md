# 13-HUMAN-RESOURCES — KPIs

## HR KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Target | Dashboard |
|-----|---------|--------|-----------|
| Employee headcount | Total active employees | Budget ±5% | HR Overview |
| Utilization rate | Billable hours / Available hours × 100 | ≥ 75% | Operations |
| Leave utilization | Leave taken / Leave entitled × 100 | < 80% | HR Overview |
| Timesheet submission rate | Submitted on time / Total × 100 | ≥ 95% | Operations |
| Timesheet approval time | Avg hours from submit to approve | ≤ 24 hours | HR Overview |
| Absenteeism rate | Absent days / Working days × 100 | ≤ 3% | HR Overview |
| Overtime ratio | Overtime hours / Total hours × 100 | ≤ 10% | Operations |
| Headcount by department | Active employees per department | Budget aligned | HR Overview |
| Turnover rate | Terminations / Avg headcount × 100 / year | ≤ 15% | Executive |

## Financial KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Refresh |
|-----|---------|---------|
| Total labor cost | Sum of salaries + overtime + benefits | Monthly |
| Labor cost per project | Sum of timesheet costs per project | Weekly |
| Labor cost % of revenue | Total labor cost / Total revenue × 100 | Monthly |
| Cost per hire | Recruitment cost / New hires | Quarterly |
| Payroll accuracy | Correct payslips / Total payslips × 100 | Monthly |
