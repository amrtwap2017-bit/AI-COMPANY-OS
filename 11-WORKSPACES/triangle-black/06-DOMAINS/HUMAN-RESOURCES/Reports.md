# 13-HUMAN-RESOURCES — Reports

| Report | Frequency | Content |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| Employee Headcount | Monthly | Total by department, employment type, status |
| Leave Summary | Monthly | Leave taken by type, department, remaining balances |
| Timesheet Overview | Weekly | Hours logged by employee, project, department |
| Attendance Summary | Monthly | Present, late, absent, early departure by employee |
| Project Labor Cost | Monthly | Labor cost allocation by project from timesheets |
| Department Budget Utilization | Monthly | Headcount and salary budget vs actual |
| Employee Turnover | Quarterly | Hires, terminations, turnover rate |
| Payroll Summary | Monthly | Total payroll by department, cost center |
| Overtime Report | Monthly | Overtime hours and cost by employee |
| Cost per Employee | Monthly | Salary, allowances, benefits, indirect costs |
