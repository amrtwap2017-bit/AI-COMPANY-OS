# 07-MAINTENANCE — Business Overview

## Context

After project handover, clients may require ongoing maintenance under warranty or service contracts. This domain manages service requests, scheduled maintenance, warranty claims, and SLA compliance.

## Key Flow

```
Project Handover → Warranty Period → Service Request → Assign → Execute → Close
                                              ↓
                                        Schedule
                                        Maintenance
                                        (preventive)
```
