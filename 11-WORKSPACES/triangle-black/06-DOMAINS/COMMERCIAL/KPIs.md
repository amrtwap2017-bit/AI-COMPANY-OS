# 01-COMMERCIAL — KPIs

## Sales KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Target | Dashboard |
|-----|---------|--------|-----------|
| Lead conversion rate | Converted leads / Total leads × 100 | ≥ 25% | Pipeline |
| Opportunity win rate | Won / (Won + Lost) × 100 | ≥ 40% | Pipeline |
| Average deal size | Total won value / Won deals | ≥ EGP 500K | Revenue |
| Sales cycle length | Avg days from lead to won | ≤ 60 days | Pipeline |
| Pipeline coverage | Total pipeline / Quota | ≥ 3x | Pipeline |
| Lead response time | Avg hours to first contact | ≤ 1 hour | Operations |
| Quotation accuracy | Quotes won / Quotes sent × 100 | ≥ 50% | Commercial |
| Average margin | Avg (quotation margin) | ≥ 30% | Commercial |

## Operations KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Target | Dashboard |
|-----|---------|--------|-----------|
| Survey-to-quote time | Avg hours from survey approval to quote sent | ≤ 24 hours | Operations |
| Quotation turnaround | Avg hours from draft to approval | ≤ 8 hours | Operations |
| Contract activation | Avg hours from signing to activation | ≤ 4 hours | Operations |
| Lead aging | Avg days in each stage | Stage-dependent | Pipeline |

## Executive KPIs (09-EXECUTIVE-INTELLIGENCE)

| KPI | Formula | Refresh |
|-----|---------|---------|
| MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue) | Sum of active contract values | Daily |
| Revenue forecast (30/60/90) | Weighted pipeline by stage probability | Daily |
| Sales velocity | (Win rate × Avg deal size) / Sales cycle length | Weekly |
| Client acquisition cost | Total sales cost / New clients | Monthly |
