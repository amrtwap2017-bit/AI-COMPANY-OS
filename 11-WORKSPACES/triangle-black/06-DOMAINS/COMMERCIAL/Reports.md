# 01-COMMERCIAL — Reports

| Report | Frequency | Content |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| Sales Pipeline | Weekly | Opportunities by stage, weighted value |
| Win/Loss Analysis | Monthly | Won/lost deals by reason, competitor |
| Lead Source Analysis | Monthly | Lead volume and conversion by source |
| Quotation Summary | Weekly | Quotes sent, approved, rejected, value |
| Sales Activity | Monthly | Calls, emails, meetings per rep |
| Contract Value | Monthly | Signed contract value, average deal size |
