# 04-SUPPLIER-MANAGEMENT — Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Supplier can self-register with company details and documents
- [ ] Supplier document storage with expiry tracking
- [ ] Approval workflow: Procurement Officer review → Manager approve/reject
- [ ] Rate card management with effective dates
- [ ] Only 'active' suppliers selectable in PO creation
- [ ] Blacklisted suppliers blocked across all modules
- [ ] Evaluation scorecard with weighted categories
- [ ] Evaluation auto-calculates total and updates tier
- [ ] Document expiry notifications trigger at 30 days
