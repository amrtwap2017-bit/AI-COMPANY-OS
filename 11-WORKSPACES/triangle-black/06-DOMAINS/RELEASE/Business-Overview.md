# 99-RELEASE — Business Overview

## Context

The Release domain plans and tracks the go-live of Triangle Black's Phase 6 business capabilities. It covers the testing strategy, user acceptance testing, training, deployment sequence, and post-launch monitoring.

## Release Phases

| Phase | Duration | Activities |
|-------|----------|------------|
| Sprint 1-2 | 4 weeks | Shared Kernel + 01-COMMERCIAL (backend + frontend) |
| Sprint 3 | 2 weeks | 02-PROJECT-DELIVERY, 03-PROCUREMENT, 04-SUPPLIER, 05-INVENTORY |
| Sprint 4 | 2 weeks | 06-FINANCIAL-CONTROL, 07-MAINTENANCE, 08-DOCUMENT-MANAGEMENT (MVR) |
| Sprint 5 | 2 weeks | 09-EXECUTIVE-INTELLIGENCE, 10-AI-COPILOTS, 11-INTEGRATIONS, 12-MOBILE |
| Sprint 6 (UAT) | 2 weeks | Testing, training, data migration, go-live |
| Post-Launch | 4 weeks | Hyper-care, bug fixes, optimization |
