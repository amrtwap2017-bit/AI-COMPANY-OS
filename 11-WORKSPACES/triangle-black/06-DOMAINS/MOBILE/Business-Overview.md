# 12-MOBILE — Business Overview

## Context

Field teams (engineers, surveyors, maintenance) need mobile access. Clients want to approve quotations and view project status from their phone. V1 focuses on a responsive web app (PWA) with core field workflows.

## V1 Mobile Scope

| App | Users | Key Features |
|-----|-------|--------------|
| Field Operations App (PWA) | Engineers, Surveyors, Maintenance | Daily reports, survey capture, NCR creation, photo upload, timesheet |
| Client Portal (responsive) | Clients | Approve quotations, view contracts, service requests |
| Offline Support | Field teams | Form data cached, sync when online |
