# 06-FINANCIAL-CONTROL — Reports

| Report | Frequency | Content |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| AR Aging | Weekly | Invoices by aging bucket (current, 30, 60, 90+) |
| AP Aging | Weekly | Supplier invoices by due date |
| Project P&L | Per project | Revenue, costs, margin by milestone |
| Revenue Report | Monthly | Recognized revenue by project/period |
| Trial Balance | Monthly | GL account balances |
| Cash Flow | Weekly | Incoming payments vs outgoing payments |
| VAT Report | Quarterly | Output VAT (invoices) - Input VAT (supplier invoices) |
