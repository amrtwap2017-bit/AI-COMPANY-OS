# 11-INTEGRATIONS — Business Overview

## Context

Triangle Black connects to external systems for communication, compliance, and data exchange. V1 focuses on essential Egypt-market integrations.

## V1 Integrations

| Integration | Purpose | Direction |
|-------------|---------|-----------|
| SMTP Email | Send quotations, invoices, notifications | Outbound |
| Egypt E-Invoice (ETA) | Submit invoices to tax authority | Outbound |
| Bank Feeds | Payment reconciliation | Inbound |
| WhatsApp API | Client notifications, lead capture | Bidirectional |
| Google/Outlook Calendar | Site survey scheduling | Bidirectional |
