# Phase 00 — Vision & Architecture

> Strategic foundation for Triangle Black — Enterprise Hospitality Engineering Platform.

## Vision Statement

**Triangle Black** is the operating system for hospitality engineering companies in Egypt. It unifies the complete client lifecycle — from lead capture to project delivery, financial control, and ongoing maintenance — into a single, integrated platform.

## The Problem

Hospitality engineering companies in Egypt currently manage operations across disconnected tools:
- **Excel sheets** for quotations and project tracking
- **WhatsApp** for client communication and approvals
- **Email** for document exchange and invoicing
- **Separate accounting software** for financial control
- **Paper-based** site surveys and handover documents

This fragmentation causes:
- Lost leads and delayed follow-ups
- Inconsistent pricing and margin erosion
- Project delays from poor coordination
- Cash flow gaps from slow invoicing
- Zero visibility into business performance

## The Solution

A unified, cloud-based platform that:
1. **Captures and tracks** every lead from every channel
2. **Automates** quotation generation with margin controls
3. **Manages** project delivery from approval to handover
4. **Controls** procurement, inventory, and supplier relationships
5. **Integrates** financial control with ETA-compliant invoicing
6. **Provides** real-time dashboards for business intelligence
7. **Works offline** on mobile for field operations

## Core Beliefs

- **Egypt first**: Built for the Egypt hospitality market, by understanding its unique dynamics (ETA regulations, banking landscape, hotel construction pipeline)
- **Revenue first**: Build what generates revenue before what saves costs
- **Startup economically**: $6-40/mo infrastructure to prove product-market fit
- **Domain expertise**: Hospitality engineering requires deep domain knowledge, not generic ERP
- **Integration practical**: Manual processes today, automated tomorrow — don't stall V1 for perfect integrations

## Target Market

- **Primary**: Hospitality engineering companies in Egypt
- **Secondary**: General MEP/construction companies in Egypt
- **Tertiary**: Hospitality engineering companies in MENA region (V2+)

## Market Size

- Egypt hospitality market: USD 21.54B (2026)
- 7.12% CAGR
- 143 hotels / 33,926 rooms in pipeline
- Growing construction and refurbishment demand
