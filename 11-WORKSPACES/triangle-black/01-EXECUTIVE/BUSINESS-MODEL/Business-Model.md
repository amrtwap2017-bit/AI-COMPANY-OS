# Phase 00 — Business Model

> How Triangle Black creates, delivers, and captures value.

## Value Creation

Triangle Black creates value by:
1. **Increasing win rates** — faster quotations, better margin visibility
2. **Reducing project delays** — coordinated procurement and resource tracking
3. **Improving cash flow** — milestone-based billing, faster invoicing
4. **Lowering operational costs** — replacing spreadsheets with automated workflows
5. **Providing business intelligence** — real-time KPIs across all operations

## Value Delivery

| Channel | Description | Priority |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| Direct sales | B2B direct to hospitality engineering firms | Primary |
| Industry referrals | Through hotel consultants and contractors | Secondary |
| Digital presence | Website, LinkedIn, industry events | Supporting |

## Revenue Model

### V1 (Months 1-12) — Subscription

| Tier | Price (EGP/mo) | Users | Features |
|------|----------------|-------|----------|
| Starter | 3,000 | 5 | Commercial + Project Delivery + Financial |
| Professional | 7,500 | 15 | All domains except AI + Mobile |
| Enterprise | 15,000 | Unlimited | Full platform including AI Copilots + Mobile |

### V2 (Months 13+) — Tiered + Transaction

| Component | Model | Notes |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| Base subscription | Monthly/Annual per tier | Same tiers as V1 |
| Transaction fee | 0.5% of invoice value | For payment gateway integration |
| AI Copilot | Per-query or bundled | Lead scoring, margin validation, NCR classification |
| Mobile offline | Premium add-on | Per field user per month |
