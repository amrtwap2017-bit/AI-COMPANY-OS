## The Opportunity

The hospitality industry in Egypt is undergoing structural transformation. Hotels are scaling rapidly, guest expectations are rising, and operational complexity is exploding — yet back-of-house engineering, maintenance, and procurement remain fragmented, paper-heavy, and disconnected from business intelligence.

Egypt's hospitality market is estimated at USD 21.54 billion (2026), growing at 7.12% CAGR to USD 30.39 billion by 2031. With 143 hotels and 33,926 rooms planned (Africa's largest pipeline), the operational management challenge is acute.

## The Company

Triangle Black is an Operational Engineering Partner — a new category that embeds itself into the operational DNA of hotel clients, managing engineering operations, procurement, maintenance, suppliers, projects, and executive reporting through a proprietary digital platform.

## Core Identity

- **Purpose:** Transform hospitality operations from fragmented and reactive to connected and data-driven
- **Vision:** Most trusted operational engineering partner across the Middle East
- **Mission:** Help hospitality businesses design, supply, build, maintain, and optimize engineering operations
- **Positioning:** Not a contractor, supplier, or software company — an Operational Engineering Partner

## Business Model

Five revenue streams:
1. Engineering Supplies (margin on materials)
2. Engineering Contracting (labor and installation)
3. Design Services (consulting and assessment)
4. Project Management (oversight fees)
5. Operational Partnership (retainer)

Future: Software Subscription, AI Services, Data Analytics

## V1 MVP

Seven modules: Public Website, CRM, Projects, Quotations, Client Portal, Executive Dashboard, Administration

## Technology

Single VPS ($25-40/mo): Ubuntu, Docker Compose, Nginx, PostgreSQL, Next.js, NestJS, Cloudflare Free

## Strategic Objective

Acquire first 5-10 hotel clients in Sharm El Sheikh. Prove the model. Expand across Egypt. Then Middle East.
