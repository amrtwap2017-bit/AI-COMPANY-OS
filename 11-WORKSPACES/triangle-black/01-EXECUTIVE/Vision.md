## Vision Statement

To become the most trusted operational engineering partner for the hospitality industry across the Middle East by combining engineering excellence, intelligent procurement, digital operations, and artificial intelligence into one integrated ecosystem.

## Vision Decomposition

| Dimension | What It Means |
|---|---|
| Most Trusted Partner | Not one of many. The recognized category leader that hotels choose first. |
| Hospitality Industry | Hotels, resorts, and hospitality real estate — not commercial or residential. |
| Across the Middle East | Starting in Sharm El Sheikh, scaling to Egypt, then UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and beyond. |
| Engineering Excellence | Deep technical competence in MEP, HVAC, electrical, plumbing, fire fighting, kitchen, laundry, pools. |
| Intelligent Procurement | Data-driven purchasing with scored suppliers, governed approvals, and spend analytics. |
| Digital Operations | Every workflow digitized, every process connected, every stakeholder on the same platform. |
| Artificial Intelligence | AI that predicts failures, optimizes procurement, generates reports, and augments human decisions. |
| One Integrated Ecosystem | Not separate tools. One platform. One source of truth. One operating system. |

## Success Looks Like (Year 10)

- 500+ hotel properties served across the Middle East
- Regional offices in Egypt, UAE, Saudi Arabia
- 90%+ client retention rate
- AI systems that predict 40%+ of maintenance needs before failure
- Platform recognized as industry standard for hospitality engineering operations
- Revenue diversified across services, software, and AI
- Triangle Black is the default answer when a hotel asks "who handles our engineering?"
