## The North Star

**Number of operational decisions made using platform data — per hotel, per month.**

## Why This Metric

| Criterion | How It Fits |
|---|---|
| Measures value, not vanity | Not logins or page views — actual decisions driven by platform intelligence |
| Grows with depth of adoption | A hotel using the platform superficially scores low; one fully embedded scores high |
| Predicts retention | Hotels making decisions from the platform data do not churn |
| Drives AI strategy | Every AI feature is justified by its contribution to this metric |
| Aligns all teams | Product, Engineering, Client Success all optimize toward the same outcome |

## Examples of "Operational Decision"

- A chief engineer approves a work order based on platform data
- A GM reviews a procurement report and adjusts budget allocation
- A maintenance schedule is optimized based on equipment history
- A supplier is selected based on platform performance scores
- An invoice is approved because it matches a PO and delivery note

## Target

- V1 Launch: 50 decisions/month across all hotels
- Year 1: 200 decisions/month
- Year 2: 1,000 decisions/month
- Year 3: 5,000+ decisions/month
