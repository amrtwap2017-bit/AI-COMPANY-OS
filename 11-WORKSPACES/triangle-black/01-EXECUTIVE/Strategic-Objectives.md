# Strategic Objectives

| Field | Value |
|---|---|

| Document Purpose | Define measurable strategic objectives for the next 5 years |
| Version | 1.0 |
| Status | Approved |

## Year 1 — Foundation

- Establish Triangle Black as operational partner for 5-10 hotels in Sharm El Sheikh
- Launch V1 platform (Marketing Website, CRM, Projects, Quotations, Client Portal, Dashboard, Admin)
- Generate first revenue through engineering supplies and contracting
- Prove the model with at least one reference client

## Year 2 — Growth

- Expand to 15-25 hotels across Sharm El Sheikh and Hurghada
- Launch V2 platform (intelligence features, procurement, AI reports)
- Establish operational partnership retainer model with 3+ clients
- Reach operational profitability

## Year 3 — Scale

- 50+ hotels across Egypt (Sharm, Hurghada, Cairo, Alexandria)
- Launch V3 platform (AI agents, supplier marketplace, benchmarking)
- Open second office in Cairo or Hurghada
- Begin Middle East exploration (UAE)

## Year 5 — Regional Leadership

- 150+ hotels across Egypt and Middle East
- Regional offices in 3 countries
- AI-driven operations as standard offering
- Platform licensing revenue stream established
- Recognized category leader

## North Star Metric

**Number of operational decisions made using platform data — per hotel, per month.**

This metric measures value (not vanity), grows with adoption, predicts retention, and aligns all teams.
