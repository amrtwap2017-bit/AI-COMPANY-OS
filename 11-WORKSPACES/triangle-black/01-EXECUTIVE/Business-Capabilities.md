## Capability Map

### Business Development
- **Marketing** — Generate leads through content, SEO, partnerships, industry presence
- **CRM** — Manage leads, opportunities, companies, and contacts through the sales pipeline
- **Quotations** — Convert opportunities into priced proposals and winning quotations
- **Contracts** — Execute and manage client agreements, SLAs, and terms

### Engineering Delivery
- **Site Survey** — Assess hotel engineering needs through physical inspection
- **Engineering Design** — Create technical solutions (MEP, HVAC, electrical, plumbing)
- **Procurement** — Source materials, equipment, and services from qualified suppliers
- **Supply** — Deliver materials to site on time and in specification
- **Contracting** — Execute installation, construction, and commissioning
- **Installation** — Deploy equipment and systems per design specifications
- **QA/QC** — Inspect and verify quality of delivered work
- **Handover** — Transfer completed work to client with documentation

### Client Success
- **Client Portal** — Provide transparent access to operations, projects, and reports
- **Reports** — Generate operational and financial reports for hotel leadership
- **Support** — Provide ongoing client assistance and issue resolution
- **Renewals** — Extend contracts and grow partnership scope

### Internal Operations
- **Finance** — Accounting, invoicing, payments, budgeting
- **HR** — People management, hiring, training, payroll
- **Knowledge** — Domain expertise, standards, research library
- **Documents** — Central document management and version control
- **Administration** — Platform governance, user management, settings

### Executive Intelligence
- **KPI** — Real-time business metrics and operational dashboards
- **Forecasting** — Revenue, resource, and project forecasting
- **Risk** — Business and operational risk identification and management
- **Strategy** — Long-term planning, competitive analysis, market intelligence
