## Why Triangle Black Exists

Triangle Black exists to transform hospitality operations from fragmented, reactive, and opaque processes into connected, data-driven, engineering-led operations that increase efficiency, transparency, profitability, and long-term asset value for hotel owners and operators.

## The Problem

Hotels today operate with:

- **Fragmented operations** — Engineering, procurement, maintenance, and projects each operate in silos with separate processes, separate records, and no shared intelligence
- **Reactive maintenance** — Equipment breaks, then gets fixed. Preventive maintenance is inconsistent. Predictive maintenance is nonexistent.
- **Opaque procurement** — Purchasing decisions are decentralized, supplier performance is unmeasured, and spend data is trapped in invoices and spreadsheets
- **Disconnected stakeholders** — Hotel owners, GMs, chief engineers, suppliers, and contractors all have partial visibility. Nobody has the full picture.
- **Lost institutional knowledge** — When a chief engineer leaves, years of operational knowledge leave with them

## The Solution

An integrated operational ecosystem where:
- Every work order is tracked
- Every purchase is governed
- Every supplier is scored
- Every asset has a history
- Every stakeholder has visibility
- Every decision is data-informed

## What Triangle Black Is NOT

- Not a contractor that shows up and leaves
- Not a supplier that delivers and invoices
- Not a software company that sells licenses
- Not a consultant that writes reports

Triangle Black IS an Operational Engineering Partner — embedded in hotel operations, accountable for outcomes, and continuously improving through data and technology.

## Purpose Never Changes

This purpose is permanent. It does not change with markets, technology, or revenue. It is the reason Triangle Black exists and will exist 20 years from now.
