# Suppliers Knowledge Base

|----------|-------------|----------|

## Supplier Evaluation Criteria
| Criteria | Weight | Scoring (1-5) |
|----------|--------|---------------|
| Price Competitiveness | 25% | 5 = Lowest in category, 1 = Highest |
| Quality | 20% | 5 = Zero defects, 1 = >10% returns |
| Delivery Reliability | 20% | 5 = 100% on-time, 1 = <60% |
| Payment Terms | 10% | 5 = Net 60+, 1 = Prepayment required |
| Responsiveness | 10% | 5 = <4 hour response, 1 = >48 hour |
| Compliance & Certifications | 10% | 5 = All required certs current |
| Financial Stability | 5% | 5 = Strong, audited |

## Supplier Tier Classification
| Tier | Score Range | Treatment |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| Preferred | 4.0 - 5.0 | First consideration, better payment terms, strategic partnership |
| Approved | 3.0 - 3.9 | Standard engagement, regular terms |
| Conditional | 2.0 - 2.9 | Limited engagement, strict inspection, short payment terms |
| Suspended | < 2.0 | No engagement until issues resolved |

## Key Supplier Information to Capture
- Company legal name and trading name
- Tax registration and commercial registration numbers
- Contact details (phone, email, address, website)
- Category and sub-category certifications
- Insurance certificates and validity dates
- Bank account details for payment
- Owner/management names and relationship notes
- Past performance records
- References from other hotel clients
