# Engineering Domain Knowledge Base

## MEP Systems Overview

MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing) systems are the core engineering infrastructure of any hotel.

### HVAC Systems
| Component | Function | Maintenance Frequency |
|-----------|----------|----------------------|
| Chiller | Building cooling | Monthly inspection, annual overhaul |
| Air Handling Unit (AHU) | Air distribution | Quarterly filter change, annual coil cleaning |
| Fan Coil Unit (FCU) | Room-level cooling | Bi-annual cleaning, filter change |
| Cooling Tower | Heat rejection | Weekly chemical treatment, annual cleaning |
| Boiler | Hot water / heating | Monthly check, annual service |
| Ductwork | Air distribution | Annual inspection, cleaning every 3-5 years |
| Exhaust Fans | Ventilation | Quarterly bearing check, annual service |
| Thermostat / BMS | Temperature control | Calibration check quarterly |

### Electrical Systems
| Component | Function | Maintenance Frequency |
|-----------|----------|----------------------|
| Main Distribution Panel | Power distribution | Annual thermographic inspection |
| Backup Generator | Emergency power | Weekly test run, monthly load test |
| UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply) | Critical load backup | Quarterly battery check |
| Transformer | Voltage regulation | Annual oil analysis |
| Lighting Control System | Automated lighting | Monthly sensor check |
| Fire Alarm Panel | Life safety | Weekly test per NFPA 72 |
| BMS (Building Management System) | Centralized control | Monthly system health check |

### Plumbing Systems
| Component | Function | Maintenance Frequency |
|-----------|----------|----------------------|
| Water Pumps | Water distribution | Quarterly seal check, annual overhaul |
| Water Heaters | Domestic hot water | Annual descaling, anode rod check |
| Sewage Pumps | Wastewater removal | Monthly check, quarterly cleaning |
| Irrigation System | Landscape watering | Weekly timer check, seasonal winterization |
| Pool Filtration | Pool water quality | Daily chemical check, weekly backwash |

### Fire Fighting Systems
| System | Standard | Inspection Frequency |
|--------|----------|---------------------|
| Sprinkler System | NFPA 13 | Quarterly visual, annual test |
| Fire Hose Reel | NFPA 14 | Monthly visual, annual pressure test |
| Fire Extinguisher | NFPA 10 | Monthly visual, annual certification |
| Smoke Detectors | NFPA 72 | Semi-annual test |
| Fire Suppression (Kitchen) | NFPA 17A | Quarterly professional inspection |
| Emergency Lighting | NFPA 101 | Monthly 30-second test, annual 90-minute test |

## Preventive Maintenance Principles
| Principle | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Time-Based | Maintenance performed at fixed intervals (daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, annually) |
| Usage-Based | Maintenance after specific run hours or cycles |
| Condition-Based | Maintenance triggered by sensor data or inspection findings |
| Predictive | AI-driven prediction of failure based on pattern analysis |
| Run-to-Failure | Acceptable for non-critical, low-cost assets with redundancy |

## Engineering Terminology
| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| MTBF | Mean Time Between Failures — reliability metric |
| MTTR | Mean Time To Repair — service speed metric |
| OEE | Overall Equipment Effectiveness — availability x performance x quality |
| CMMS | Computerized Maintenance Management System |
| SLA | Service Level Agreement — response and resolution time commitments |
| O&M | Operations and Maintenance manual |
| BMS | Building Management System |
| SCADA | Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition |
