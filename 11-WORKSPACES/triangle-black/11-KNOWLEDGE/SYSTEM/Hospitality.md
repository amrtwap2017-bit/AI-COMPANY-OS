# Hospitality Industry Knowledge Base

## Overview
The hospitality industry encompasses businesses that provide accommodation, food, beverage, and related services to travelers and guests. For Triangle Black, the focus is on hotel engineering operations — the back-of-house systems that keep a property running.

## Hotel Organizational Structure

### Departments
| Department | Function | Triangle Black Relevance |
|------------|----------|-------------------------|
| General Manager | Overall property leadership | Primary client stakeholder |
| Engineering | Maintenance, MEP, physical plant | Core operational partner |
| Housekeeping | Room cleaning, linen, public areas | Work order request source |
| Food & Beverage | Kitchens, restaurants, bars | Procurement and maintenance |
| Front Office | Reservations, check-in, concierge | Guest communication |
| Procurement | Purchasing and supplier management | Direct collaboration |
| Finance | Accounting, budgeting, controls | Payment and reporting |
| Sales & Marketing | Revenue generation | Lead source |

### Engineering Department Hierarchy
| Role | Reports To | Responsibilities |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| Director of Engineering | GM | Full engineering operations |
| Assistant Chief Engineer | Director of Engineering | Shift operations, team supervision |
| Shift Engineer | Assistant Chief Engineer | Shift handover, emergency response |
| Technician (HVAC, Electrical, Plumbing) | Shift Engineer | Task execution, maintenance |
| Storekeeper | Chief Engineer | Parts inventory, receiving |

## Industry KPIs
| KPI | Description | Benchmark |
|-----|-------------|-----------|
| Guest Satisfaction Score | Engineering-related guest feedback | > 90% positive |
| Work Order Completion Rate | % completed within SLA | > 95% |
| Preventive Maintenance Compliance | % of scheduled PM completed | > 90% |
| Mean Time To Repair (MTTR) | Average time to resolve | < 4 hours (critical) |
| Energy Cost Per Occupied Room | Energy efficiency measure | Benchmark per property type |
| Water Consumption Per Guest Night | Water efficiency | Benchmark per region |
| Procurement Spend Per Room | Annual procurement efficiency | Benchmark per category |

## Hospitality Segments
| Segment | Characteristics | Examples |
|---------|----------------|----------|
| Luxury Resorts | 300+ rooms, full amenities, international brands | Four Seasons, Marriott |
| Business Hotels | City center, meeting facilities, corporate guests | Hilton, Sheraton |
| Boutique Hotels | Small, unique design, personalized service | Independent |
| All-Inclusive Resorts | Package pricing, extensive F&B, entertainment | Rixos, Iberotel |
| Extended Stay | Apartment-style, long-term guests | Residence Inn |

## Operational Challenges
| Challenge | Impact |
|-----------|--------|
| High energy costs | 15-30% of operating budget |
| Aging infrastructure | Increasing maintenance costs |
| Skilled labor shortage | Difficulty retaining qualified engineers |
| Brand standard compliance | Penalties for non-compliance |
| Environmental regulations | ESG reporting requirements |
| Seasonal demand fluctuation | Workforce and budget planning difficulty |
