# Frequently Asked Questions

## About Triangle Black

**Q: What is Triangle Black?**
A: Triangle Black is an Operational Engineering Partner for the hospitality industry. We manage hotel engineering operations, procurement ecosystems, maintenance workflows, supplier networks, project execution, and executive reporting through a proprietary digital platform.

**Q: What makes Triangle Black different from a contractor?**
A: Contractors perform discrete tasks. Triangle Black embeds in your operations as a long-term partner. We own the process, the platform, the data, and the outcomes — not just individual work orders.

**Q: Is Triangle Black a software company?**
A: No. Triangle Black is an operational services company powered by a proprietary platform. We don't sell software licenses — we sell operational partnership and results.

**Q: What types of hotels does Triangle Black work with?**
A: We specialize in large luxury resorts (300-1,500 rooms), hotel groups with multiple properties, and internationally branded hotels entering the Egypt/Middle East market.

**Q: Where does Triangle Black operate?**
A: Headquarters in Sharm El Sheikh, Egypt. Expanding to Egypt-wide, then Middle East, and globally.

## About the Platform

**Q: What modules are included in the platform?**
A: Engineering Operations (work orders), Preventive Maintenance, Procurement Management, Supplier Management, Project Management, Executive Intelligence Dashboard, Client Portal, and AI Assistant.

**Q: Can our hotel team use the platform?**
A: Yes. The Client Portal gives your GM, Engineering Director, Procurement Manager, and other stakeholders direct visibility into operations, procurement status, and reports.

**Q: Is the platform available in Arabic?**
A: Yes. The platform supports both English and Arabic interfaces.

**Q: Can we access the platform on mobile?**
A: Yes. Field engineers have a mobile app for task execution. Management has mobile access to dashboards and alerts.

**Q: How is data security handled?**
A: Security-by-design. All data encrypted in transit and at rest. Role-based access control. Full audit trail. ISO 27001 framework alignment.

## Onboarding and Implementation

**Q: How long does it take to onboard a property?**
A: Standard onboarding is 4-6 weeks: 1 week pre-work assessment, 2 weeks asset registration and data import, 1 week team training, 1 week parallel run.

**Q: What do we need to provide for onboarding?**
A: Asset inventory, current supplier list, current maintenance schedules, organizational chart, and access to existing documentation (contracts, manuals).

**Q: How is the team trained?**
A: Role-based training sessions: 2-hour management overview workshop, 4-hour engineering team hands-on training, 2-hour procurement process training. Follow-up support for 2 weeks post go-live.

## Commercial

**Q: How is Triangle Black paid?**
A: Revenue model includes monthly operational retainer, platform access fee, project execution fees, and procurement management fees.

**Q: What are the contract terms?**
A: Standard contract: 12-36 months with monthly billing. Pilot programs available for first-time clients.

**Q: Can we start with a pilot?**
A: Yes. We offer 90-day pilot programs for qualified properties with defined success metrics and guaranteed outcomes.
