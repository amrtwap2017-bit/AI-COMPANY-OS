## Industry Best Practices
| Practice | Standard Reference | Application |
|----------|-------------------|-------------|
| Preventive maintenance scheduling | ISO 55001, NFPA 25 | All assets |
| Energy monitoring and targeting | ISO 50001, ASHRAE 90.1 | Monthly reporting |
| Water treatment and quality | WHO Guidelines, ECP 302 | Pools, cooling towers |
| Waste management | ISO 14001 | Property-wide |
| Contractor safety management | ISO 45001 | All third-party work |
| Chemical handling storage | NFPA 45, OSHA HAZCOM | Maintenance chemicals |
| Lockout/tagout procedures | NFPA 70E, OSHA 1910.147 | Electrical maintenance |
