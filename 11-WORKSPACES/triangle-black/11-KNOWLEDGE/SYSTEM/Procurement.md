# Procurement Domain Knowledge Base

## Procurement Categories
| Category | Sub-Categories | Strategic Importance |
|----------|----------------|---------------------|
| MEP Equipment | Chillers, pumps, AHUs, boilers, generators | High — capital equipment |
| Electrical | Cables, panels, breakers, lighting, switches | Medium — consumable + capital |
| Plumbing | Pipes, fittings, valves, fixtures, pumps | Medium — consumable + capital |
| HVAC Parts | Compressors, coils, fans, filters, refrigerants | High — operational critical |
| Fire Fighting | Sprinklers, extinguishers, detectors, suppression | High — safety, regulatory |
| Spare Parts | Bearings, belts, seals, gaskets, motors | Medium — operational |
| Consumables | Cleaning chemicals, lubricants, gaskets | Low — high volume |
| FF&E | Furniture, fixtures, equipment | High — project-based |
| Tools & Equipment | Power tools, testing equipment, safety gear | Low — periodic |
| Services | Maintenance contracts, consulting, training | Medium — relationship |

## Procurement Lifecycle
| Stage | Key Activities | Documents |
|-------|---------------|-----------|
| Need Identification | Requisition raised, specifications defined | Purchase Requisition |
| Sourcing | Supplier identification, RFQ distribution | RFQ Document |
| Supplier Selection | Quote comparison, negotiation, selection | Quote Comparison Sheet |
| Ordering | PO creation, supplier confirmation | Purchase Order |
| Logistics | Shipping tracking, customs clearance | Shipping Documents |
| Receiving | Goods receipt, quality check, GRN creation | Goods Receipt Note |
| Invoicing | Invoice submission, three-way match | Supplier Invoice |
| Payment | Payment processing, reconciliation | Payment Confirmation |

## Sourcing Strategy
| Category | Strategy | Supplier Relationship |
|----------|----------|----------------------|
| Strategic Items | Long-term contracts, partnership | Strategic partnership |
| Leverage Items | Competitive bidding, annual negotiation | Preferred supplier |
| Bottleneck Items | Multiple sources, safety stock | Managed relationship |
| Routine Items | Automated procurement, consolidated orders | Transactional |

## Supplier Evaluation Criteria
| Criteria | Weight | Data Source |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| Price Competitiveness | 25% | Quote comparison |
| Delivery Reliability | 20% | On-time delivery history |
| Quality | 20% | Defect rate, return rate |
| Payment Terms | 10% | Net days offered |
| Responsiveness | 10% | RFQ response time, communication |
| Compliance | 10% | Certifications, regulatory status |
| Financial Stability | 5% | Credit check, financial statements |
