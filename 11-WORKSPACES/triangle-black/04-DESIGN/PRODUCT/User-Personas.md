# User Personas

## Persona 1: Ahmed — Hotel General Manager

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Age | 45-55 |
| Background | 20+ years in hospitality; worked up from F&B or rooms |
| Tech Literacy | Moderate — uses email, WhatsApp, basic Excel |
| Goals | Reduce guest complaints; control costs; keep owner happy |
| Pain Points | No visibility into engineering; relies on Chief Engineer's word; emergency costs blow budget |
| Platform Use | Dashboard (weekly), approve quotations, review reports |
| Success Metric | "I can see what's happening without asking anyone." |

**Quote:** *"I don't need to know how the AC works. I need to know it works, and what it costs."*

---

## Persona 2: Karim — Chief Engineer

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Age | 35-50 |
| Background | Engineering degree or technical diploma; 10+ years in hotel engineering |
| Tech Literacy | Medium — uses BMS, excel, some cloud tools |
| Goals | Keep equipment running; document maintenance; reduce emergency callouts |
| Pain Points | Chasing suppliers for quotes; no documented history; firefighting daily |
| Platform Use | Daily — submit RFQs, track projects, upload documents, manage work |
| Success Metric | "I spend less time on WhatsApp and more time on actual engineering." |

**Quote:** *"I have 15 chiller suppliers on WhatsApp and none of them answer when I need them."*

---

## Persona 3: Mona — Purchasing Manager

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Age | 30-45 |
| Background | Supply chain or hospitality management |
| Tech Literacy | Medium-high — uses procurement systems, Excel expert |
| Goals | Get best price; reduce procurement cycle time; maintain supplier relationships |
| Pain Points | No centralized purchase history; manual comparison; approval delays |
| Platform Use | Daily — create RFQs, compare quotations, issue POs (V2) |
| Success Metric | "I can see all quotes side by side and make a decision in minutes." |

**Quote:** *"I spent 3 hours yesterday comparing 8 quotations on two monitors. There has to be a better way."*

---

## Persona 4: Yasser — Financial Controller

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Age | 40-55 |
| Background | Accounting or finance degree; CPA or equivalent |
| Tech Literacy | High — uses ERP, accounting software, advanced Excel |
| Goals | Budget control; accurate forecasting; audit-ready records |
| Pain Points | No visibility into committed spend; manual invoice matching; no budget vs. actual tracking |
| Platform Use | Weekly — review quotation values, check project budgets, download reports |
| Success Metric | "I know exactly what we've committed to spend before the invoice arrives." |

**Quote:** *"I don't approve anything without a PO, and right now half our spend arrives without warning."*

---

## Persona 5: Samir — Hotel Owner / Investor

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Age | 50-70 |
| Background | Business owner; may have multiple properties |
| Tech Literacy | Low — uses phone, email, relies on GM for details |
| Goals | Asset value protection; ROI; compliance; reputation |
| Pain Points | No direct visibility; relies entirely on GM reports; surprised by capital calls |
| Platform Use | Monthly — login to view executive dashboard, review quarterly reports |
| Success Metric | "I trust that my asset is being managed properly without being involved day-to-day." |

**Quote:** *"I don't want to manage the hotel. I want to know it's being managed well."*

---

## Persona 6: Nadia — Internal Admin (Triangle Black)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Age | 25-35 |
| Background | Business administration or operations |
| Tech Literacy | High — uses multiple SaaS tools daily |
| Goals | Keep the system running; onboard clients; support team |
| Pain Points | Manual user setup; no audit trail; repetitive administrative work |
| Platform Use | Daily — create users, assign roles, configure settings, monitor usage |
| Success Metric | "I can onboard a new client in under 2 hours without manual steps." |

**Quote:** *"I spend half my week creating accounts and resetting passwords. Let me focus on client success."*

---

## Persona 7: Tarek — Field Engineer (Triangle Black)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Age | 25-40 |
| Background | Technical diploma or engineering degree; hands-on technician |
| Tech Literacy | Low-medium — uses smartphone, WhatsApp, basic apps |
| Goals | Do quality work; document what was done; get paid accurately |
| Pain Points | Paper forms; no access to job history; photos lost in phone gallery |
| Platform Use | Daily from phone — view job details, upload photos, mark complete |
| Success Metric | "I finish my job, take photos on the app, and it's done. No paperwork." |

**Quote:** *"I take before/after photos on my phone, but nobody ever sees them because I forget to email them."*
