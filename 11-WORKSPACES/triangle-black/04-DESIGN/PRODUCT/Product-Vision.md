# Product Vision

## Vision Statement

**By 2031, Triangle Black will be the operating system for hospitality engineering across the Middle East — a unified platform that connects hotel owners, operators, engineers, suppliers, and contractors into a single data-driven ecosystem.**

Every operational decision — from procurement to maintenance to compliance — will be informed, tracked, and optimized through the platform.

## 5-Year Product Horizons

### Horizon 1 — Foundation (Year 1)
**Theme:** Replace chaos with clarity

The platform eliminates the fragmented tools (WhatsApp, spreadsheets, email, paper) that hotels use today to manage engineering operations. V1 is a connected suite of essential modules that proves the value of a single source of truth.

Key outcomes:
- All hotel engineering data in one place
- Transparent procurement and project tracking
- Professional client reporting replacing ad-hoc communication
- Internal operational efficiency gains

### Horizon 2 — Intelligence (Year 2-3)
**Theme:** Turn data into decisions

With structured data from Horizon 1, the platform introduces AI-powered insights, predictive maintenance signals, procurement optimization, and automated reporting. The platform shifts from being a record-keeping tool to a decision-support system.

Key outcomes:
- Predictive maintenance reduces unplanned downtime by 30%
- Procurement analytics reduce spend by 15-20%
- AI-generated reports eliminate manual reporting work
- Benchmarking data provides competitive context

### Horizon 3 — Ecosystem (Year 3-5)
**Theme:** Network effects multiply value

The platform becomes a multi-sided marketplace connecting hotels with vetted suppliers, contractors, and engineers. Data from hundreds of hotels creates industry-wide benchmarks. AI agents autonomously manage routine procurement and scheduling.

Key outcomes:
- Supplier marketplace with automated RFQ matching
- Cross-property inventory sharing and consolidated procurement
- Industry benchmark reports as a revenue stream
- AI agents handling 50%+ of routine operational decisions

## Platform Principles

1. **Revenue-first** — Every feature must directly enable revenue generation or retention
2. **Client-obsessed** — Hotel clients are the primary users; internal efficiency is secondary
3. **Data as asset** — Structured data is the platform's long-term moat
4. **Mobile-ready** — Field engineers and hotel staff operate from phones
5. **Offline-resilient** — Hotels in remote areas cannot rely on consistent connectivity
6. **Configurable, not customized** — One platform, configurable per client, never forked

## Strategic Moats

| Moat | Description | Timeline |
|------|-------------|----------|

| Switching Costs | Deep integration into hotel operations makes replacement painful | Year 1+ |

| Domain Expertise | Hospitality engineering knowledge embedded in platform logic | Year 1+ |
