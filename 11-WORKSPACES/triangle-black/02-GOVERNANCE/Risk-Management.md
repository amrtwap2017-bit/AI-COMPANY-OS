# Risk Management

## Risk Categories

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Market | Slower hotel adoption than projected, competitor response |
| Operational | Delivery quality issues, supply chain disruptions |
| Technical | Platform reliability, data loss, security breach |
| Financial | Cash flow constraints, unexpected infrastructure costs |
| Regulatory | Compliance changes, import/export restrictions |

## Risk Assessment Matrix

| Probability | Impact | Rating |
|---|---|---|
| High | High | CRITICAL — Immediate action required |
| High | Medium | HIGH — Mitigation plan required |
| Medium | High | HIGH — Monitor and plan |
| Medium | Medium | MEDIUM — Standard monitoring |
| Low | High | MEDIUM — Contingency plan |
| Low | Medium | LOW — Accept |
| Any | Low | LOW — Accept |
