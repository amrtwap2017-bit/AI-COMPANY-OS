# 2. CORE BUSINESS PROBLEM

Traditional hotel engineering operations are often:

* spreadsheet-driven
* dependent on individual experience
* reactive rather than planned
* fragmented across departments
* weak in asset lifecycle management
* weak in preventive maintenance discipline
* difficult to audit
* difficult to measure
* dependent on suppliers
* disconnected from financial analysis
* rich in operational data but poor in operational intelligence

The central problem is not simply:

> "maintenance costs are high."

The deeper problem is:

> **Organizations collect large amounts of operational data but fail to convert that data into controlled workflows, measurable performance, transparent decisions, and long-term asset intelligence.**

Triangle Black exists to solve this problem.

# 13. TENANCY MODEL

Triangle Black is a multi-tenant platform.

Tenant isolation is mandatory.

Security principles:

```text
JWT identity
      ↓
organization identity
      ↓
authorization
      ↓
resource ownership
      ↓
tenant-scoped query
```

Never trust:

* client-provided organization IDs
* arbitrary headers
* URL parameters alone
* frontend authorization
* hidden UI controls

Tenant boundaries must be enforced server-side.
